# Provenance

Job Application Builder was independently authored for a private Nova Emergent source edition. Its exact inclusion in Nova Free 3.0.0 is authorized by INCLUSION-NOTICE.md and the containing product license.

## Product authority

Approved goal:

> Build a truthful application for one specific vacancy from the posting, work history, and existing materials.

Approved completion state:

> A role-matched resume, cover letter, application answers, evidence-to-claim check, work-sample guidance, unresolved-claim list, and submission checklist are ready for user review.

## Private source lineage

Career Strategist — CAREER-X T1 v5 was read completely and hash-confirmed as private construction input. Selected praxis informed vacancy delta analysis, parse-safe artifacts, truthful terminology, evidence-bearing claims, work-sample guidance, consistency, and explainability. Persona adoption, wrappers, promotion, career campaigns, LinkedIn work, outreach, interview practice, offer negotiation, and unsupported screening scores were excluded.

The shipped source register preserves the source name, digest, rights state, transformation, and derivative destinations without exposing private workstation paths. The original source is not shipped and is not a runtime dependency.

Those sources do not make the package legal advice or establish the legitimacy of a specific vacancy or employer.
