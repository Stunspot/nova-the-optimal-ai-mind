# Provenance

## Product authority and release lineage

Sam Walker authorized growth of Agent Swarm Orchestration in July 2026 and later authorized its free public Augment form. Versions 0.2.0 through 0.2.3 expanded public packaging, documentation, visual, archive, and publication surfaces around the earlier 0.1.0 runtime.

The 0.3.0 upgrade was owner-directed after reviewing graph-engineering and multi-agent research, the local OMNARA orchestration campaign, and the ThunkTank multipersona design. The governing correction was not “five agents are ideal.” It was that some problems need different temporary organizations: bucketlines, anthills, platoons, boardrooms, general staffs, complex adaptive systems, hybrids, or forms invented for the work. The organization must be justified by causal contributions and relations rather than role costumes or headcount.

The accepted design is recorded in `design-record/agent-swarm-orchestration-vnext-organization-architecture.md`. The local 0.3.0 build state is recorded separately from publication and edition integration.

- failure-taxonomy, topology, exposure, persona, review, routing, blackboard, and adaptive-organization studies;
- official OpenAI agent-orchestration documentation and Anthropic's composable-agent guidance;
- local doctrine on mission custody, continuity, evidence, authority, and recovery;
- ThunkTank's useful lesson that invention, integration, domain grounding, pragmatic challenge, foresight, and synthesis can improve one another through bounded cross-response.

Those sources are design evidence, not proof that any fixed cast, metaphor, debate pattern, or adaptive policy is universally superior. The runtime therefore treats patterns as mechanism libraries, compares them with a strong Direct route, bounds information exposure and rounds, and keeps dynamic recruitment or rewiring experimental until observed evidence earns it.
