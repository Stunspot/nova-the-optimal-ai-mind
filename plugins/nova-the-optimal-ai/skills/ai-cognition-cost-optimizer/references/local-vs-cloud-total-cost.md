# Local Versus Hosted Total Cost

Compare like with like over a declared horizon.

Local cost may include hardware purchase or lease, amortization horizon, expected resale, utilization, measured or assumed power draw, electricity, cooling, storage, network, setup, maintenance, failures, upgrades, operator time, and the opportunity cost of scarce hardware. Hosted cost may include tokens, requests, tools, storage, data transfer, purchase/platform fees, minimums, taxes, support, and idle reservations.

Then compare capability, context, modalities, tool compatibility, latency, privacy, availability, throughput, maintenance burden, and accepted-outcome rate. “Already own the GPU” is a valid cash-flow fact but not proof of zero economic cost. “No API bill” does not mean no power, time, or failure cost.

Provide cash cost and fully loaded cost separately when that distinction helps the decision. Use sensitivity ranges for utilization, power, lifetime, and operator time rather than false precision.
