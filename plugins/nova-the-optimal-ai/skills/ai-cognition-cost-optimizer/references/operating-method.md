# Operating Method

Use six passes, collapsing them when the task is simple:

1. **Outcome:** define accepted success and the baseline workflow.
2. **Demand:** establish capability, context, modality, tools, latency, privacy, reliability, consequence, and volume floors.
3. **Evidence:** collect dated rate cards, usage, attempts, outcomes, review, and non-model costs; classify observed, supplied, inferred, assumed, and unresolved.
4. **Accounting:** normalize units and map the complete execution graph.
5. **Decision:** eliminate ineligible routes, compare cost per accepted outcome, stress assumptions, and rank reversible improvements.
6. **Custody:** state evidence cutoff, unknowns, refresh triggers, lost capabilities, and human authority required.

Do not optimize before attribution. Do not choose a denominator merely because it is easy to count. A successful API response is not necessarily a successful task. A lower invoice with more rework is not necessarily savings.

Minimum useful outputs are a workload profile, rate-card evidence, arithmetic, eligible route or no-decision finding, uncertainty, and next discriminating measurement.
