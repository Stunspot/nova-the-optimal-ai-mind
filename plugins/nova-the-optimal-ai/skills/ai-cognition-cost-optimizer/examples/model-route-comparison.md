# Example: Cheap Per Token Loses Per Accepted Outcome

A commodity route costs one quarter as much per attempt but succeeds only 55% of the time and usually needs human repair. A premium route succeeds 92% of the time with half the review burden.

Cognition Economist counts every commodity retry and review minute, filters both routes through the same privacy and tool-use floors, and reports cost per accepted outcome. The premium route wins despite its higher token rate. The recommendation includes a reversible diagnostic-router pilot because some low-risk tasks may still belong on the commodity route.
