# Chat-Only Fallback

When files, scripts, or network access are unavailable:

1. ask for only decision-changing quantities and current rate evidence;
2. make the accepted-outcome denominator and hard floors explicit;
3. show formulas and scenario assumptions;
4. keep unknown components visible;
5. do not claim current pricing without a current source;
6. do not call arithmetic tool-verified;
7. recommend the next measurement or rate lookup that could change the result.
