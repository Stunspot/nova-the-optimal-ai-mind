# Northbridge heat-resilience brief

This is synthetic demonstration material. Northbridge and all values are fictional.

The city measured afternoon surface temperatures at four neighborhood sites during one illustrative July week. The supplied table contains the medians. The planning team wants residents to understand that heat exposure is shaped by shade, surface, and route conditions—not only by the citywide forecast.

The intended response is practical: notice which everyday routes concentrate heat, then prioritize shade and cool-surface interventions where exposure and foot traffic overlap. This demonstration does not establish medical risk, equity impacts, intervention cost, or real-world effectiveness.

The planning team describes three linked levers:

1. Shade reduces direct solar exposure along walking routes.
2. Surface choices change how much heat a route stores and re-radiates.
3. Route timing and access determine who encounters the hottest conditions.

All claims and measurements in this file are illustrative, not observed facts.
