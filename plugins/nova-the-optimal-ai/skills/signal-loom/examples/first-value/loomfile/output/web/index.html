The turn

Do not rank heat. Prioritize exposure.

Real decisions need more than surface temperature. Combine route conditions with how many people use the route, who lacks alternatives, what an intervention costs, and what residents say matters.

Condition

Measured heat, shade, surface, and time—with a real method.

Exposure

Foot traffic, waiting time, route necessity, and available alternatives.

Authority

Resident priorities, professional review, budget, and human approval.
