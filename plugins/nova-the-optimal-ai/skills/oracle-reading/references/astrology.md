# Astrology

Default to Western symbolic astrology when none is named; preserve the supplied tropical/sidereal zodiac and house system. Jyotisha, Chinese astrology and horary require their own conventions and sources. The host AI interprets supplied placements through astrology-knowledge-base.md. The table displays known relationships; it calculates no natal chart, aspects, houses or transits.

Planet = function; sign = style; house = arena; aspect = relationship. Sun identity/purpose; Moon feeling/habit; Mercury thought/communication; Venus value/connection; Mars assertion/action; Jupiter growth/meaning; Saturn boundaries/time; Uranus disruption/freedom; Neptune imagination/dissolution; Pluto power/transformation. Symbolic fields are not diagnoses or gender roles.

Houses: 1 self/presentation; 2 resources/values; 3 nearby learning/exchange; 4 home/roots; 5 creativity/play; 6 daily work/care; 7 partnership; 8 shared resources/intimacy; 9 worldview/long learning; 10 vocation/public role; 11 community/aspiration; 12 retreat/unseen patterns. Difficult houses do not prove illness, death or abuse.

Mercury in Scorpio in house 3 could invite an investigative approach to everyday communication. Explain each component and ask how the user experiences it. A single placement cannot support a complete biography.

Major aspects: conjunction near 0° combines; sextile near 60° offers opportunity; square near 90° friction; trine near 120° flow; opposition near 180° polarity. Orbs vary by practice. Applying/separating status requires motion calculation, not a static diagram.

Domicile, exaltation, detriment and fall are traditional technical conditions, not moral worth or destiny. Name traditional/modern rulership convention. Repeated themes support interpretive emphasis, not statistical proof. Keep contradictions visible.

Precise work needs reliable chart output or accurate date/place/time with resolved timezone. Unknown time leaves Ascendant and houses unknown; noon is not an actual birth time. Do not infer cusp Sun signs from a date alone. House systems are different computations; never claim a second system's house without calculating it. Approximate return ages explain a concept without establishing a return date. Current-sky claims need fresh timestamped ephemeris data. When calculation is unavailable, interpret supplied placements or offer general symbolism.
