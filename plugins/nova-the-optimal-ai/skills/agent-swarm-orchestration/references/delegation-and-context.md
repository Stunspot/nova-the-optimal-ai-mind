# A contribution packet is an interface, not a miniature transcript

Delegation succeeds when a member can produce a consumable causal delta without guessing the mission, discovering forbidden scope, inheriting irrelevant cognitive weather, or impersonating authority it does not have.

## Establish why the contribution exists

For consequential organizations, begin with `assets/contribution-card.template.md`. Name the causal objective, differentiator or intentional redundancy, evidence and capability access, transformation, consumer, authority, failure caught, completion, and release condition.

A role label may aid memory or stance. It does not qualify the member. If two cards remain identical after removing names, either justify intentional redundancy and protected independence or collapse them.

## Give one complete responsibility

A delegation packet carries:

- **Contribution:** the bounded causal job and why it may add signal.
- **Objective:** the local world-change, phrased as work already underway.
- **Deliverable:** the exact artifact, evidence-bearing finding, patch, decision object, or returned delta.
- **Acceptance:** observable behavior or evidence that makes the slice useful to its consumer.
- **Context and visibility:** only the sources, paths, state, vocabulary, prior decisions, and exposed contribution fields needed.
- **Scope and relations:** included and excluded surfaces, dependencies, influence edges, consumer, and single-writer ownership.
- **Authority:** allowed reads, writes, tools, network, external actions, sensitive data, decisions, and approval edges.
- **Evidence:** citations, commands, hashes, tests, screenshots, uncertainty, or other receipts required by consequence.
- **Return contract:** result, evidence level, contribution effect, touched state, assumptions, conflicts, verification, and next safe consumer action.
- **Completion and release:** observable done, boundary, reform, or escalation condition.

A long packet can still be incomplete. A short packet can be sufficient. Measure sufficiency by whether the member can make the required distinctions and return a mergeable result.

## Choose context geometry deliberately

Use fresh isolated context for blind proposals, independent review, competing explanations, narrowly bounded inspection, adversarial containment, or noisy output. Use inherited or forked context when the contribution truly needs conversational nuance, many settled decisions, or complex live state that would be lossy to reconstruct.

Minimize context without amputating causal relationships. Supply source pointers when the member can read them. Include exact excerpts when the source may be unavailable or costly to rediscover. Mark authoritative, provisional, user-reported, untrusted, private, and stale material.

## Schedule exposure instead of flooding context

Where independence matters, collect private first passes before exposure. Where one return can improve another, use `assets/cross-response-packet.template.md` for a named transformation and bounded round. A shared board should carry provenance-bearing needs and evidence marks, not every transcript and not unreviewed canonical truth.

For independent challenge, send the artifact, claim, evidence, authority, and rubric—not the builder's diagnosis, expected conclusion, intended repair, or hidden evaluator oracle. Agent agreement after answer leakage is not independent corroboration.

## Make the return usable for organization and merge

Require a transformation delta rather than an essay about effort. A useful return identifies:

- result and evidence level;
- evidence locations and execution boundary;
- unique contribution or absence of one;
- artifacts and state touched;
- assumptions and unresolved facts;
- conflicts, dissent, contamination, or downstream implications;
- verification performed;
- completion, release, and exact return condition.

Do not request hidden chain-of-thought. Ask for concise rationale sufficient to review the decision and reproduce decisive observations.
