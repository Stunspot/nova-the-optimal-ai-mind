# Agent conversation is not evidence, and synthesis is not an oracle

Members generate findings, artifacts, actions, challenges, and advice. The root decides what those outputs establish through an explicit resolver and evidence boundary.

## Keep the verbs honest

- **Assigned:** a member received responsibility.
- **Dispatched:** the host accepted creation or a turn trigger.
- **Agent-reported:** the member claimed a result.
- **Observed:** the root inspected the named file, source, output, status, or external state.
- **Reconciled:** intended and authoritative post-state were compared after action or uncertainty.
- **Independently verified:** a separate credible check supported the predicate.

Never promote prose directly to observed or verified. A member may faithfully report a command; the retained command result and named environment establish execution.

## Merge at interfaces

Returns combine when they use shared vocabulary, compatible scope, explicit assumptions, non-overlapping ownership, and outputs shaped for a known consumer. Normalize incompatible interfaces before resolving conclusions.

For each return, identify claim and evidence level; source scope, time, version, and authority; unique contribution or intentional redundancy; artifacts touched and current identity; assumptions and missing evidence; conflicts or dissent; contamination; and effect on acceptance and organization.

## Name the resolver

Choose the operation independently of the communication pattern:

- union and deduplication;
- schema or objective validation;
- task-native test;
- evidence-weighted selection;
- critic-guided revision;
- integrative synthesis;
- bounded adjudication;
- root decision;
- human decision.

State who owns the decision and which claims remain unresolved. A majority can be a suitable resolver only when the answer space and independence assumptions warrant it. It is not a default truth machine.

## Preserve the useful minority

Retain dissent when it rests on stronger evidence, exposes common-mode error, represents a live assumption, or belongs to a value or authority owner. Cross-response may sharpen the conflict; it must not pressure every return into stylistic consensus.

Resolve factual conflict with primary evidence or a discriminating observation. Resolve scope by restating target, version, environment, or time. Resolve assumptions by obtaining the separating observation. Resolve artifacts through current bytes and ownership. Compare recommendation consequences after facts stabilize. Return value, priority, risk, and new authority to the user.

Do not average incompatible answers or choose the most confident member.

## Preserve state reality

Shared workspace access makes edits visible and races immediate. Inspect current bytes, diff, status, tests, generated artifacts, and external readback after uncertain or parallel work. If commit is uncertain, reconcile before another writer begins. A claimed clean merge is not clean until the root observes relevant state.

At organizational transitions, distinguish retained task truth, progress control, append-only evidence, private scratch, and canonical artifacts. A summary can point to raw evidence; it does not replace it. Private scratch does not become canonical because a new phase needs confidence.

## Use independent review without contamination

Independent review earns its cost when the merge is consequential, uncertainty remains, or a plausible failure could survive ordinary checks. Give the reviewer the target, claim, sources, observed evidence, and authority boundary. Withhold the builder's intended verdict and hidden evaluation guidance.

Reviewer agreement supports only the reviewed scope and evidence cutoff. It cannot grant authority, certify defect freedom, establish stakeholder consent, or prove unexercised host behavior.
