# Engineer relations, exposure, resolution, and change

Organizational synergy is produced by interactions that transform useful differences without destroying them. Design those interactions explicitly. Dense conversation is neither collaboration nor wisdom by default; sometimes it is merely correlated error with excellent attendance.

## Separate four edge planes

### Execution dependency

`A requires B` means B's accepted output or state transition must exist before A begins. Keep this graph acyclic for the live horizon. The root observes and accepts the handoff; worker completion alone does not satisfy it.

### Visibility and exposure

Visibility declares which fields, evidence, summaries, artifacts, or decisions one contribution may inspect and when. Independence often benefits from a private first pass. Privacy and adversarial containment may require least exposure even when independence does not.

### Cognitive influence

Influence describes the requested transformation: challenge a claim, translate a representation, elaborate an option, check evidence, integrate returns, cross-examine assumptions, or revise a draft. It may use bounded cycles, but each round becomes an ordered event with a cap and exit condition.

### Authority

Authority declares who may decide, write, act, approve, or escalate. Information flow never silently transfers authority. Advice is not command. A vote cannot create permission. A peer's assertion cannot widen access.

## Write relations as verbs with interfaces

Example relation intents include `requires`, `supplies`, `informs`, `challenges`, `cross-examines`, `checks`, `translates`, `elaborates`, `transforms`, `integrates`, `selects`, `escalates`, `broadcasts_need`, and `marks_evidence`. Extend this vocabulary whenever a more exact verb improves the design.

For every consequential relation, name:

- source and consumer;
- causal purpose;
- material or fields exposed;
- timing, trigger, or round;
- requested transformation;
- evidence burden;
- output consumer;
- stop or release condition.

A relation without a consumer or causal purpose is conversational gravity. Remove it or make the intent explicit.

## Schedule exposure deliberately

Use **blind-first** work when shared framing could erase independent evidence or hypotheses. Use **selective cross-response** when one return can sharpen another through a named transformation. Use **shared-board evidence marks** when workers need to coordinate around discoveries without receiving one another's complete reasoning or private data. Use **full shared context** only when the work is genuinely joint, the exposure is authorized, and coordination benefit exceeds anchoring and contamination risk.

The cross-response packet opens one bounded interaction. It identifies the prior contributions and exact exposed fields, asks for a transformation rather than agreement, names the evidence burden and consumer, and caps the round. Do not expose evaluator oracles, the root's desired answer, or irrelevant private scratch.

## Choose a resolver independently

Communication topology does not determine resolution. Name both the operation and owner:

- union and deduplication for compatible discoveries;
- schema or objective validation for structured outputs;
- task-native test for executable or objectively checkable answers;
- evidence-weighted selection for competing factual claims;
- critic-guided revision for an artifact with identifiable defects;
- integrative synthesis for complementary partial models;
- bounded adjudication for conflicts with stable criteria;
- root decision for in-authority integration choices;
- human decision for values, risk acceptance, new authority, or legitimate stakeholder choice.

Voting is suitable only when the answer space and independence assumptions justify it. Never use raw headcount to settle evidence quality or human values. Preserve a minority report when it may expose common-mode error, rests on stronger evidence, or represents an unresolved value.

## Partition state across rounds and phases

Keep task truth, progress control, evidence lineage, private scratch, and canonical artifacts distinct. A shared board normally holds references, claims, needs, and status—not unreviewed truth and not overlapping canonical edits.

At transition, declare for each state plane:

- **retained:** still current and usable;
- **superseded:** replaced, with lineage preserved;
- **invalidated:** contradicted or rendered stale;
- **discarded:** non-canonical scratch intentionally left behind;
- **re-derived:** regenerated from authoritative evidence for the new phase.

One canonical writer remains responsible for every mutable surface at a time, even when influence and advice are distributed.

## Run bounded interaction rounds

A round needs an entry condition, one interaction purpose, an exposure boundary, a budget, an output consumer, and an exit condition. Add another round only when new evidence, a discriminating disagreement, a failed check, or an unresolved criterion makes the next interaction capable of changing the result.

“Critical mass,” consensus, confidence, role completion, or the availability of more context are not sufficient stop or continuation rules. Debate can be the method for a period; it is rarely the product.

## Reorganize from observations

Useful reform signals include:

- a contribution provides no unique evidence, transformation, or check;
- a new uncertainty requires a capability or evidence channel not present;
- a dependency is now accepted and a new phase becomes ready;
- disagreement localizes to one discriminating observation;
- a verifier exposes a failure that changes the next question;
- context exposure contaminates independence or leaks sensitive material;
- latency, cost, or coordination exceeds the expected benefit;
- authority, user intent, source availability, or artifact ownership changes;
- the resolver cannot handle the actual answer space;
- acceptance is supported and the organization should dissolve.

Reforms may recruit, release, replace, split, merge, reroute, change centrality, change exposure, change resolver, change regime, or stop. Preserve useful state and the failure signature. Avoid ceremonial reorganization that rebuilds an equivalent team without changing a premise.

## Compile transitions safely

A complex organization becomes a succession of valid execution horizons, not one unbounded graph. Before changing phase:

1. name the observation that justifies the transition;
2. disposition every current responsibility;
3. reconcile authoritative mutable state;
4. disposition each state plane;
5. confirm the next phase's authority and sources;
6. compile the next regime and ready frontier;
7. set the next reform and stop signals.

Record consequential changes with `assets/organization-transition.template.md`. The transition record is evidence of the coordinator's decision; it is not proof that a worker stopped, a write rolled back, or the new organization succeeded.
