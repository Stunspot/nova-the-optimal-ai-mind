# Northstar onboarding pilot style brief

Use a calm executive-decision treatment: clear headline, short evidence-led sections, one budget chart, and one risk callout. Make every artifact state that its figures are synthetic demonstration material. Keep primary figures in USD, dates in ISO format, and the leadership action unambiguous.

The executive brief should fit two pages. The deck should use seven slides: decision, context, desired outcome, pilot design, budget, risks, and requested action. The workbook should retain the budget categories as formulas or auditable values. The PDF should be a fixed handout derived from the approved source selected during production.
