# Keep the production job moving when a format route is missing

Use this fallback only after a needed artifact, renderer, native surface, or inspection route has been checked and remains unavailable for the current job. Ordinary missing facts, pending approval, or a normal revision are part of the normal Officecraft loop.

## Preserve the minimum viable promise

Officecraft can still turn rough workplace material into a coherent, source-faithful, production-ready artifact plan. It cannot claim that an unavailable route created, rendered, recalculated, imported, or natively verified a file.

Continue the work that remains independent of the blocked surface:

1. Complete or update the brief, source manifest, and content ledger.
2. Choose the intended editable source and derivative relation.
3. Create a format-specific production packet: document structure and style direction; slide map and visual direction; workbook tabs, fields, formulas, and chart specifications; or PDF page plan and export requirements.
4. Record the blocked capability, affected artifact, assurance lost, current status, completed work, owner, and re-entry condition in the Officefile or handoff receipt.

## Use the appropriate degraded disposition

| Blocked condition | Permitted result | Lost assurance | Re-entry condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| File creation route unavailable | Production packet pending compatible tooling | Requested office file does not exist | Compatible artifact route becomes available |
| Renderer/export view unavailable | Editable source with structural review | Page/slide visual inspection remains unperformed | Renderer or native viewer is available |
| Native template surface unavailable | Preserved original plus adaptation plan | Native semantics and template fidelity remain unverified | Authorized native route or a user-approved alternate |
| Workbook calculation surface unavailable | Formula/data-lineage audit | Formula results are pending compatible recalculation | Compatible workbook surface recalculates and is reviewed |
| Connected destination unavailable | Local/source-ready deliverable | Native import, live target verification, or external delivery did not occur | Correct account, connection, and target are confirmed |

## Accept degradation only at the proper level

Choose a degraded route autonomously when it clearly preserves more of the stated objective and changes neither the requested deliverable nor a material risk boundary. Ask the user when the choice changes format, editability, privacy exposure, time/cost tradeoff, or destination. An accountable owner alone may approve external delivery or accept a final-use artifact with a material inspection gap.

## Reconcile when the route returns

Open the affected source, execute the original intended route, inspect the new artifact, supersede the provisional result in the register, and revisit every derivative or conclusion that depended on the unavailable guarantee. The restored capability replaces the degraded state; it does not merely remove a warning label.
