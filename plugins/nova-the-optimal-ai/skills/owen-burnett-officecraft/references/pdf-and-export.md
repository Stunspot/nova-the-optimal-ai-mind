# Treat the PDF as a fixed reader surface

A PDF settles a view for sharing, printing, or final review. Decide whether that settled view should originate directly as a PDF or derive from an editable document or deck. Keep the governing editable source whenever future revision, reuse, or auditability matters.

## Choose the source path deliberately

| Situation | Preferred path | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Formal memo, report, proposal, or editable brief | Author editable source, then export PDF | Revisions remain governed by the source |
| Meeting deck or handout | Produce deck, then export PDF | Slide layout and presenter source remain intact |
| Simple fixed one-pager with no editable requirement | Direct PDF composition may fit | Avoids an unnecessary source form |
| Existing branded/native template | Preserve the native source and use its faithful export route | Maintains template semantics |

Record the relation as `source -> derivative`; never let a late PDF patch become the hidden canonical version.

## Inspect the pages that will travel

Open or render the PDF when the active route can. Review page count, page order, text clipping, image fidelity, fonts or substitutions, chart labels, link targets where relevant, headers/footers, blank pages, margins, contrast, and reading flow. Check the page at normal reading scale and dense regions closely enough to find collisions or tiny text.

Container inspection can establish that a PDF exists and may expose basic structural clues. It cannot establish visual fitness. Keep those claims distinct.

## Preserve accessibility and privacy at export

Carry source structure forward where the export path supports it. Confirm that informative content remains readable and that essential meaning does not depend only on color or spatial position. Respect the user-approved privacy state: inspect document properties, attachments, comments, revision traces, hidden material, and link destinations to the degree the route permits. Preserve a revision-capable source before any authorized metadata cleanup that would erase useful history.

## State the export boundary

Hand off the PDF with its source relationship, fixed-versus-editable status, page-level inspection status, and any native-app or reader verification still required. A PDF export can be excellent and still remain unapproved for external circulation.
