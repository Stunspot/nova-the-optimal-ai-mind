# Research brief

Verbatim inquiry: Does a falling local tide-gauge trend contradict rising global mean sea level, and what must be checked before comparing them?

Audience: a careful reader interpreting two apparently inconsistent charts. Purpose: choose the correct comparison before inferring contradiction. Scope: measurement definitions in selected official NOAA and NASA product pages, accessed 2026-10-06. Exclude estimating a station trend, validating a satellite algorithm, projecting flood levels or deciding construction. No paid, private or authenticated access. Success is a bounded conceptual answer and the missing comparison evidence, not a source quota or a long report.

Rival accounts: actual incompatible evidence; different measured quantities/references; different periods or processing. The obtained product definitions can test the second account. They cannot diagnose a named station without data.
