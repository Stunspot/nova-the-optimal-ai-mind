## 1.3.0 - Campaign-wide evidence terrain, 2026-09-28

Update against the accepted 1.2.0 room. The default atlas now keeps every recorded claim and its cited sources in one navigable topology. Selecting a claim exposes its full recorded trail; selecting a source reveals its reach across the argument and opens the native reading note. Structural observations call out unlinked claims, unreviewed judgments, source concentration and sources outside the claim network without treating those measures as evidentiary support. Filters, empty views and large citation sets preserve orientation and access to the underlying records.

Aperture is a charcoal and copper instrument with an adjacent evidence inspector; Marginalia is an ochre editorial spread with the interpretation beneath the full map; Signal is a compact olive console with orthogonal traces and a lower readout. All three use the same native campaign files. The research doctrine, campaign schema, validation and conflict-safe saving remain unchanged. This is a local product and shelf update, not evidence of a new public announcement or fresh-host qualification.

## 1.1.0 - local campaign room candidate

Baseline: accepted standalone 1.0.1. This is a feature update because it adds a supported visual research workflow and portable Open action, while retaining the native campaign format and research doctrine.

The campaign room creates and imports native vaults, edits the question, hypotheses, research brief, sources and full-reading notes, claims, coverage, contradictions, report outline and section drafts; saves with native validation and revision conflict checks; exports complete vaults and direct next-pass handoffs. The original candidate supplied three skins; the repair above replaces their presentation with two contextual compositions. State lives outside the package; repeated Open reconnects the matching source/data instance, safely selects a free loopback port after collisions, and works again after restart. Browser/API access uses an instance token and rejects foreign Origin/Host requests.

The local candidate includes customer guidance and a build receipt. macOS launch syntax is shipped but not exercised on a macOS host. A room cannot perform research or establish citation entailment without the research agent and evidence review.
