# Evidence and documentation boundary — OMNARA Deep Research 1.5.0

The customer path starts with the research question and moves into the report-first Reading Room. The room guide explains cited passages, retained notes versus originals, two-source comparison, search and return, refresh and review changes, history, appearance preferences and private offline export. New research still uses the OMNARA skill; users are not asked to fill research records to begin reading.

The complete native research skill, persona, evidence doctrine, promptcraft, campaign schema, validators, source tools, review contract, themes and worked inquiry remain in the package. Author-declared review is attributed and content-bound; it is not presented as independent confirmation. The included source notes are not described as complete originals.

The standalone package validator checks delivered files, hashes, JSON records and local links. It does not authenticate the publisher, establish source truth or semantic entailment, or qualify every host. Local browser use does not establish universal usability or installation. Native macOS execution and broad independent usability qualification are not claimed. See [the Reading Room guide](../docs/CAMPAIGN-ROOM.md), [validation boundary](../docs/VALIDATION.md) and [release notes](../RELEASE-NOTES.md).
