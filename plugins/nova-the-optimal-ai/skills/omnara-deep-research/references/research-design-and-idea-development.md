# Develop research ideas until they meet evidence

Use this craft for an authorized research proposal, experiment plan, or research-to-manuscript commission. Ordinary literature inquiry remains on its existing campaign route. A plan describes work; it grants no authority to run compute, collect sensitive data, recruit participants or change production systems.

Start from the live question and a small, dated domain brief: problem, constraints, established baselines with primary sources and versions, unresolved disagreements, tempting but unsupported intuitions, and small experiments that could distinguish explanations. Keep source assertions, author inference and candidate proposals distinguishable. Draft additions stay outside a maintained knowledge canon until its owner reviews them. Prefer the existing campaign vault and knowledge steward to a new parallel store.

Generate from useful combinations of a specific source idea and a domain opportunity. Name the source and its relevant passage, the target problem, the transferred causal or mathematical relation, required assumptions and where the mapping breaks. A shared metaphor is insufficient. Record no fit when no defensible transfer exists; do not manufacture a proposal for every pair. Keep a promising unconventional candidate when it has a testable mechanism even if reviewers find it unfamiliar. Preserve selected-source lineage, revisions and consequential rejected candidates with reasons so resumption does not rediscover a known dead end.

When alternatives need scoring, report scored candidates over eligible candidates, failed or unavailable assessments, and the meaning of the score. Unassessed candidates stay unassessed; they are not zeroes, average fives or hidden exclusions. Multi-model judgments are fallible assessments, not independent empirical evidence. Establish any consequential quorum against the intended eligible reviewer set before execution; do not shrink the denominator after failures or count the same underlying model behind multiple aliases as independent votes. Preserve substantive dissent and unparsed responses.

Translate a retained idea into a falsifiable hypothesis: the claim, population or setting, comparator, primary outcome, minimally meaningful difference where defensible, and the observation that would count against it. Include a secondary measure when it catches an obvious way to game the primary outcome, such as accuracy purchased with unacceptable latency or average gains that conceal a harmed subgroup. Choose replication, uncertainty analysis and domain methods for the actual inference; model agreement is not a substitute.

Separate a feasibility pilot from the main study. The pilot asks whether the data, implementation, measurements and resource assumptions work. Passing it does not establish the scientific claim. Decide the main study's outcomes, comparisons, exclusions, stopping conditions and resources before inspecting its results. Pilot-informed changes are documented before the main study; later changes produce an explicitly revised or exploratory analysis. Preserve the original hypothesis and prior results instead of silently moving the goalposts.

Set bounds that mean something for this work: wall time, money, compute, data, attempts, revisions and who can authorize expansion. Account for the full call graph before multi-model expansion: with m generators each reviewed by m reviewers, one direction costs m generation calls plus m squared review calls, before search, revision and retries. Include payload size, concurrent calls and fallbacks in the forecast. Checkpoint completed candidate or experiment units so a retry resumes the affected unit rather than replaying the campaign. Written budgets remain plans unless an observed executor enforces them.

Check baseline currentness against the actual as-of date and primary sources when the comparison depends on being current. Preserve the original baseline and classify it as current, stale, unsuitable or unverified with a reason. A replacement changes the experimental design: inspect data compatibility, assumptions, measurement, cost and the hypothesis before accepting it. Reassess the affected design and novelty claim after any freshness rewrite. Avoid hard-coded calendar dates and automatic substitution.

Return the best supportable proposal, a bounded pilot/main plan when earned, and its manuscript implications. A well-designed negative result is useful. If no idea survives, preserve that outcome and the smallest evidence-producing next move.
