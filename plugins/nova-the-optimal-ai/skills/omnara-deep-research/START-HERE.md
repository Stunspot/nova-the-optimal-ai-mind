# Understand the evidence behind your research

Start with the question, not a form. Ask OMNARA to investigate the issue for the audience or decision that matters. Share ordinary documents, passages or links when you have them. OMNARA handles research planning, evidence gathering and report synthesis; its native ledgers remain available for careful review, but you do not need to author them to begin.

## Open the Reading Room

With Python 3.10 or newer, double-click **Open.cmd** on Windows or run `python3 workspace/open.py` on macOS or Linux. The room looks in the existing OMNARA research home, using Nova active estate research when available; portable installations fall back to Documents/OMNARA Campaigns. Use `OMNARA_HOME` or `python workspace/open.py --data-root "your folder"` to select another existing research location. The application and the research home are separate; keep the home when updating the application.

Open an inquiry by its title and question, or choose the clearly labeled included sea-level example. The saved report is the natural starting point. Its citations lead to the matching source record and retained reading note. Read the note's recorded scope and limitations, then open a supported official web link if you need the live original. A retained note is not a complete archived webpage.

## Follow and test the argument

Choose two source records to read alongside each other. Their dates, locations, methods and recorded limits help you see what their comparison can establish. Search for a phrase across the inquiry; selecting a result takes you to the passage or note, and Back to report returns you to the reading place. The room displays the author's declared review and makes its scope clear; it does not independently verify the claim.

After a researcher changes the native report or evidence, use **Refresh inquiry**. The current report and review binding reload together. A changed report is identified as changed since review, while the prior saved report and notes remain in a historical view. Your room appearance and reading place are stored separately from the research records.

Use **Save reading copy** to preview and download a private HTML copy with the report, citations and retained notes. Open the downloaded file in a browser without a network connection. Its external URLs remain references, and no unbundled originals are included. **Prepare a chat request** produces text you can copy into a research conversation; it is not sent automatically.

For the complete native inquiry method, validation and campaign file details, use the [OMNARA skill](SKILL.md) and [documentation index](docs/README.md). The Reading Room is for understanding saved research; it does not perform web research, create evidence or declare a review.
