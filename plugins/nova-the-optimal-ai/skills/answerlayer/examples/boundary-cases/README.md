# Boundary cases

- A current article about an old event is not a new delta unless a threshold or answer changed.
- A final rule with a future effective date may change preparation now without changing current legal status.
- Two sources that differ by jurisdiction are not necessarily in conflict.
- A validator pass does not authorize a patch.
- A human review note does not prove execution or outcome verification.
- A 2025 Almanac report cannot establish 2026 currentness without fresh authority.
- “No meaningful deltas” is preferable to filling the report with weak signals.
