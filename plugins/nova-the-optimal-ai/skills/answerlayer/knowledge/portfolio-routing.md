# Portfolio routing

Use AnswerLayer when the central question is: **Does this new evidence change an existing answer, assumption, policy, or workflow?**

Route to the Current Intelligence Observatory when the primary need is broad OSINT gathering, source discovery, evidence packets, entity/event mapping, or presentation of an investigative case.

Route to OMNARA Deep Research when the primary need is a large, citation-heavy research campaign, literature or source synthesis, extensive report, or deep investigation across many sources.

AnswerLayer may consume outputs from either. It owns the admission gate, baseline diff, operational patch, recheck logic, and decision-integrity record. It should not imitate their collection or report scale.

Use a domain specialist or qualified professional when correctness depends on legal, medical, financial, engineering, security, or other accountable expertise. AnswerLayer can preserve the evidence and decision object; it does not acquire that professional authority.
