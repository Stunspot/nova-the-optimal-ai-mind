# Route by the user's work

Use AnswerLayer when an existing answer, policy or assumption must be reconsidered against new evidence. An open-ended investigation can begin with research; a continuing public-source watch can use Observatory or the user's chosen monitoring capability. Bring the resulting evidence back to the specific decision rather than forcing the user to understand internal routing.

If there is no baseline, help draft the bounded initial answer within existing authority and label its cutoff/unknowns. For a simple one-off question, answer directly. Persistent ledger work is justified by continuity and governance needs, not by invoking the skill name.
