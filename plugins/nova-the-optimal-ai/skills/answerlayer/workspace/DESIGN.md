# Deciding how an answer should change when facts change

A conversation can propose an update; a persistent answer desk makes the exact current wording, proposed wording, evidence, exclusions and reopening conditions inspectable together. The unchanged answer is the fixed datum. Selecting a proposal changes the comparison seam, never adoption or publication.

## Environments

Strata: near-black geological stage, a fixed answer stratum, an exact before/after seam and a lower evidence layer. Switchyard: dark blue enamel, engineered rails, a horizontal change index and adjacent current/proposed wording with labeled decision lanes.

## Information and behavior

Original JSON text, unknown fields and large numeric literals round-trip exactly. Native validation checks structure only. Edited working files require a discard decision before replacement; explicit download clears the local edited flag. Human adoption, approval and publication remain independent recorded states.

## Observed acceptance

The overnight repair was rendered and inspected in Windows Chromium at 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844. Each environment, the empty state and representative opened records were reviewed. Native checks and isolated browser fixtures establish the tested state and data behavior; no claim is made for participant usability, screen-reader acceptance or other platforms.
