# Current practice register

Cutoff: 2026-08-14
Scope: bounded safety, automated-screening uncertainty, disability, accommodation, and job-scam guidance

## Primary official sources

### FTC job-scam guidance

- [How to spot the latest job scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/12/how-spot-latest-job-scams)
- [Job scammers are looking to hire you](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/07/job-scammers-are-looking-hire-you)
- [That job offer text is probably a scam](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/04/job-offer-text-probably-scam)

Retained boundary: requests for payment, equipment purchases, fake checks, early financial or identity information, and unofficial or unexpected recruiter channels are stop-and-verify signals. Verify using contact information independently known to be legitimate. No single indicator proves legitimacy or fraud.

### EEOC employment selection and disability

- [Disability Discrimination and Employment Decisions](https://www.eeoc.gov/disability-discrimination-and-employment-decisions)
- [Employment Tests and Selection Procedures](https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/employment-tests-and-selection-procedures)

Retained boundary: hiring and selection procedures can implicate federal anti-discrimination law; disability-related inquiries and medical examinations have stage-specific limits; reasonable accommodation can apply to applicants. The Augment does not infer disability, decide legal applicability, or answer protected or medical questions for the user.

### ADA.gov algorithmic hiring guidance

- [Algorithms, Artificial Intelligence, and Disability Discrimination in Hiring](https://www.ada.gov/resources/ai-guidance/)

Retained boundary: automated hiring tools can screen out qualified applicants with disabilities, and accessible alternatives or process adjustments may be required. The Augment therefore makes no universal parsing, ranking, interview, or selection promise and keeps accommodation decisions user-owned.

## Recheck triggers

Refresh this register when the package changes its protected-information, accommodation, automated-screening, fraud, submission, or employer-contact behavior; when a cited official route materially changes; or before making a claim beyond the bounded statements in this register.
