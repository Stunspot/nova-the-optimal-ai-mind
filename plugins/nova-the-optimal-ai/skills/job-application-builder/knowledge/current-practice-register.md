# Current practice: bounded primary-source navigation

Reviewed 2026-10-07. These are United States sources, not universal employment rules or an assessment of a particular employer. The prior 2026-08-14 register remains in history/current-practice-register-20260814.md. Recheck the applicable authority when a consequential legal or current-policy question arises.

[FTC Job Scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams) describes payment-for-work promises and fake-check transfer patterns. Use these as reasons to verify a suspicious process through independently obtained official contacts before disclosing sensitive information; a lack of complaints does not prove legitimacy.

[EEOC Pre-Employment Inquiries and Disability](https://www.eeoc.gov/pre-employment-inquiries-and-disability) distinguishes generally prohibited pre-offer disability inquiries from limited accommodation questions and later-stage rules. Preserve the exact question, stage and jurisdiction instead of declaring every accommodation inquiry unlawful or inferring a disability answer.

[DOJ guidance on AI and disability in hiring](https://www.ada.gov/resources/ai-guidance/) explains that hiring technologies can screen out qualified applicants and discusses accommodations/accessibility. It does not support a universal ATS score or a promise that this package prevents discrimination.

No external employer, application portal or model was contacted during these source reads. Product guidance is bounded drafting and evidence handling, not legal representation or a live policy monitor.
