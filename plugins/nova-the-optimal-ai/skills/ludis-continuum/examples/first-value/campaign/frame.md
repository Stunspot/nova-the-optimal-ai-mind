# The Bell Below Bracken

Bracken survives by forgetting. When the drowned bell begins ringing beneath the peat, three powers move: the Memory Ferrymen want an old toll paid; the Reed Court wants the tower sealed; the Mayor's Circle wants proof destroyed. The characters decide which debts count, what the town deserves to remember, and whether the bell is a prison or a witness.

The near horizon is one descent, one bargain, and one public consequence. No prepared scene requires betrayal.
