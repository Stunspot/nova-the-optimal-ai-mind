# GM packet

## Active factions

- Memory Ferrymen, clock 2/6: collect the first volunteered memory; leverage: safe passage.
- Reed Court, clock 1/6: collapse the lower stair; leverage: town labor and flood maps.
- Mayor's Circle, clock 3/6: remove the old registry; leverage: law and public trust.

## Playable location

The bell tower descends through a tilted vestry, a drowned stair, and an air pocket beneath the bell. Water marks show something large climbed upward recently.

## Encounter

The Ferryman at the drowned stair asks for a memory before lowering the chain bridge. Players may bargain with another truth, find the maintenance ledge, or expose the Mayor's altered registry. The black water, missing handrail, and snapped chain telegraph a fall. Success opens a route and changes a faction clock. Failure costs position or information, not the campaign.

NPCs: Iven Reed, a civic engineer protecting a lie for defensible reasons; Sella Vane, a Ferryman who remembers every toll except her own.

Rules uncertainty: whether forced movement triggers the stair reaction is unresolved because the edition and authoritative wording were not supplied. Make and record a table ruling before play.

Canon dispute: the 80-year sinking and 30-year hearing cannot both be literal under present evidence. Do not resolve offscreen.
