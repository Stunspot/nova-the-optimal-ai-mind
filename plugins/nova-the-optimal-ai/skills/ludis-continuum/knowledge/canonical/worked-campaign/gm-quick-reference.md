## 🔄 Encounter Phases Summary

**Phase 1: Investigation**
- Skill checks reveal glyph function, ledger secrets, and Lira’s mental state.

**Phase 2: Breach**
- Trigger: Touching glyph, 15 minutes pass, or natural 1 on Arcana/Investigation.
- 3 masked assassins phase in via the glyph.

**Phase 3: Combat**
- Assassin targets Lira directly.
- Initiates disrupt PCs and apply pressure.
- Glyph surges on Round 3 (narrative-only spatial distortion).

**Phase 4: Aftermath**
- If Lira survives → players guided to Act II options.
- If Lira dies → choose a thread blindly; darker tone, more risk.

---

## 💥 Glyph Surge – Round 3

- Spatial instability: doubled shadows, flickering vision, bent space.
- No mechanical effect—emphasize mood and destabilization.
