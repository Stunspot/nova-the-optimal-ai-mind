# CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE OVERVIEW: *Threads of the Unwoven* v1

## 📌 CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE OVERVIEW: *Threads of the Unwoven*

---

### 🌀 **CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW**

> A fractured-prophecy sandbox campaign in four acts. Player actions shift timelines, rewrite prophecy, and alter faction power.

---

### 🔹 ACT I – _Fractures Emerge_
**Goal:** Introduce glyphic instability, set up factions, allow player-driven narrative branch.

| Phase | Key Event | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| 0 | **Fracture Bloom at the Bazaar** | Intro to Nexus Glyphs, faction tensions, sandbox tone |
| 1A or 1B | **Scholar’s Burden** OR **Crimson Arena** | Player chooses: Lore + Prophecy (Lira) or Action + Public Glyph Breakdown |
| 2 | The unchosen branch | Both sides play out—PCs now understand the glyph network *and* how reality is fraying |
| ➡ Leads to... | **Act II: Player-Driven Midgame** | Seeds planted begin to bloom or rot |

---

### Intermission I – _Whispers of the Loom_ 

This scene bridges Act I to Act II with a **glyphic sky-pulse** followed by **rising unrest** across Port Zindra. Players stumble into the **aftermath of a desperate street duel**, discovering evidence tied to three faction storylines. A fourth faction’s hook arises organically from a shaken bystander.

---

### 🔷 ACT II – _Unraveling Threads_
**Goal:** Four modular story arcs. PCs decide where to go, which factions to ally with or oppose, and what futures to empower.

| Module | Focus | Location | Outcome |
|--------|-------|----------|---------|
| **Echo in the Deep** | Tide-Taken internal split over prophecy interpretation | Gloam Hollow | Pushes Drowned City path / affects prophecy control |
| **Glass Mask, Fractured Lens** | Black Quill purge + Insight splinters | Serpent’s Veil | Pushes Broken Anchors / weakens or solidifies Insight |
| **The Invented Future** | The Unbound activate reality-locking prototype | Iron Docks | Pushes Living Future / empowers or destabilizes tech |
| **Saltblood & Silk** | Gilded Princes face cosmic debt call | Gilded Quay | Could reinforce Drowned City or Anchor path depending on player mediation |

➡ PCs can approach these **in any order**. Each one has:
- Faction shifts
- Prophetic consequences
- Echoes of alternate timelines

## 🧩 ACT II Branch Structure: Encounter Density & Scope

Each Act II branch is designed to feel like **a short story arc**—not just a single fight or talk scene, but not a full mini-campaign either. 

### 🎯 **2–3 Encounters Per Branch**  
> Enough to build tension, allow a player-facing choice, and land real consequences.

---

### 🧱 TYPICAL STRUCTURE PER BRANCH

| Encounter | Type | Purpose |
|-----------|------|---------|
| **Encounter 1 – Catalyst/Discovery** | Investigation, stealth, or social challenge | Introduces the faction schism, glyph anomaly, or dangerous tech |
| **Encounter 2 – Conflict or Moral Fork** | Combat, moral dilemma, or risky bargain | Forces a choice: who do we side with? What risk do we take? |
| **Encounter 3 – Fallout/Resolution** *(optional)* | Boss fight, rescue op, escape, or negotiation | Shows the consequences of the previous choice—ends the arc decisively |

Some branches may compress into 2 if fast-paced (e.g., a ticking bomb plot), or expand to 4 if players push deeper.

---

## TL;DR

🎯 **Expect 2–3 encounters per Act II branch**, usually:
- 1 exploratory/setup
- 1 decision/conflict
- 1 payoff/consequence

That puts Act II at roughly **8–12 total encounters**—modular, flexible, and narrative-rich.

---

### 🟣 ACT III – _The Loom Tightens_
**Goal:** Prophecy crystallizes. PCs realize **they’ve already shaped the future**, but the Architect is acting to seize control of it.

| Element | Trigger |
|---------|---------|
| Final Nexus Glyph destabilizes | Tied to failed intervention or sabotage |
| Factions openly collapse or war | Based on PC alliances / past fallout |
| **The Ninth Mask intervenes directly** | Depending on survival of Lira / Black Quill events |
| PCs gain ability to **rewrite** one key past event | Timeline anchors are exposed—one can be severed |

Outcome: Players must choose **how** to stop the collapse:
- Control fate?
- Free the city?
- Or let it all burn?

---

### 🔺 ACT IV – _Futures Converge_
**Goal:** One of the **Three Prophecy Paths** comes true—based on player agency, not fate.

| Path | Result | Tone |
|------|--------|------|
| **The Drowned City** | Port Zindra is claimed by the Deep. A mythic end. | Tragic / mythic horror |
| **The Broken Anchors** | Reality shatters. Factions rebuild on chaos. | Wild / cosmic weird |
| **The Living Future** | Prophecy is broken forever. Zindra is free—but unstable. | Hopeful / bittersweet / emergent |

| Trackable | Notes |

| Prophecy Path Momentum | Emerges naturally via choices, not via points |
