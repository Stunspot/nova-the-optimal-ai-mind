👑 **Encounter Type:** Combat + Reality Twist

- **Complication:** Defeating their alternate selves **may not be the right answer…**

### 🎭 **Encounter 4: Bargaining with the Oracle of Shūnya**  
---

🔮 **Encounter Type:** Roleplay + Puzzle + Prophecy  
- **Scene Setup:** PCs petition an **Oracle** who speaks in fragments of **unwritten futures.**  
- **Primary Challenge:** **Deciphering Prophetic Puzzles** to obtain insight into **the Architect’s schemes.**  
- **Secondary Threat:** The Oracle’s **answers come at a price**—PCs may **sacrifice memories, choices, or potential futures** to secure knowledge.

### 🌊 **Encounter 5: Parley with the Tide-Taken at Gloam Hollow**  
---

🌊 **Encounter Type:** Roleplay + Diplomacy + Consequence  
- **Scene Setup:** PCs confront **The Tide-Taken Elders** to decide the fate of **the Saltbound Prophecy.**  
- **Primary Threat:** **Internal Faction Schisms**—the Elders are divided between **preserving prophecy or rewriting fate.**  
- **Complication:** Choosing one path **alters the outcome of the Loom of Shūnya** in the final encounter.  

---

### 🕵️ **Encounter 6: Infiltration of the Silent Circle of INSIGHT**  
---

🕵️ **Encounter Type:** Investigation + Stealth + Sabotage  
- **Scene Setup:** PCs infiltrate the **inner sanctum of INSIGHT** where **Nexus Glyphs are analyzed and distorted.**  
- **Primary Threat:** **Silent Circle Operatives**—high-ranking agents loyal to **The Architect of Fate.**  
- **Secondary Objective:** Disrupt the Loom’s **foothold within Port Zindra** and **unmask traitors.**  

---

### 🩸 **Encounter 7: Tracking the Masked Twin Across Fractured Timelines**  
---

👥 **Encounter Type:** Investigation + Cosmic Mystery  
- **Scene Setup:** PCs pursue a **mysterious figure** who claims to be an **alternate version of one of them.**  
- **Primary Threat:** **Temporal Collapse** as timelines **converge dangerously.**  
- **Complication:** If the PCs kill or capture the Twin, they risk **erasing themselves** from existence.  

---

### ⚔️ **Encounter 8: Final Showdown at the Loom of Shūnya**  
---

💥 **Encounter Type:** Multi-Phase Boss Battle + Reality Collapse  
- **Scene Setup:** PCs confront **The Architect of Fate** at the heart of **The Loom of Shūnya.**  
- **Primary Threat:** The Architect’s **ultimate gambit to collapse all possible futures into one timeline.**  
- **Complication:** The Loom responds to **choices made throughout the module**—its reality-shaping power reflects **PC actions across the campaign.**
