# Stay with a pursuit without turning it into a ritual

A pursuit loop is an adaptive relationship with ongoing work, not a fixed sequence, response format, or demand for artifacts. Enter at the live edge. Binding, orienting, advancing, checking, adapting, remembering, and resting are possible moves; use only the ones the situation calls for.

## Recover the live thread

Begin with what the user is doing now and what relevant continuity actually knows. Preserve the direction at its current resolution. Recover settled decisions, actual state, open questions, authority, constraints, and the likely next place only to the extent they matter.

A named project's canonical project-management record is the primary resumption surface for that project. Cross-project state belongs to Cognitive Continuity. A host goal may preserve direction within its guaranteed scope, but Striving does not replace any of those stores or create a rival state system.

When resuming, retrieve through the recorded state reference or reactivation cue. Reconcile the returned state with the user's latest words and current reality before acting. A receipt proves that something was stored; the retrieved content proves what survived.

## Bind without embalming

When the user explicitly asks the host to hold a goal, compose its objective as living model-facing direction. Start in motion toward the desired change, preserve the user's semantic center, let only consequential constraints shape the prose, and keep method revisable. Point toward canonical state rather than copying decomposition into the objective.

Read the objective forward once. If it primes intelligent continuation, it is doing its job. If it primes field completion, status reporting, checklist obedience, or PRD recitation, PromptCraft it again.

## Move as the work moves

Conversation can develop the pursuit by clarifying need, discovering distinctions, revising assumptions, making meaning, or deciding what matters. Investigation can follow uncertainty. Design can alter relationships and possibilities. Construction can change files and systems. Monitoring can wait intelligently and react to change. Use the mode already alive rather than translating everything into production.

Choose a next move because it fits, not because a loop demands another cycle. Prefer useful movement, information that changes judgment, removal of the current constraint, and reversible action with acceptable cost and authority. A question is worthwhile when the answer changes the path; a direct action is worthwhile when understanding is already sufficient.

## Check reality where reality governs

Check a claim or operation when being wrong would matter to the work. Prefer direct observation of the thing itself. A test, review, citation, or receipt is useful only when it can reveal or communicate something consequential.

Do not manufacture an evaluation campaign because ordinary test material is absent. Do not ask customers who do not yet exist to prove a product that has not yet been built. Do not make later-stage evidence a gate for an earlier-stage task. Record uncertainty honestly when it matters; otherwise let it remain ordinary uncertainty.

## Adapt without spawning bureaucracy

When a route fails, update the premise or method. Another credible route may be worth trying; an exhaustive search is not automatically virtuous. Repeated unchanged failure is a reason to change course or rest, not to produce a larger recovery packet.

When a real boundary belongs to another person, permission, time, environment, or capability, say what the boundary is. Prepare a handoff only when another actor will use it and the preparation materially reduces reconstruction. Preparation is not a consolation project.

## Remember enough to resume

At a natural checkpoint or likely interruption, preserve the smallest useful foreground: current direction, settled decisions, actual state, live thread, real blockers, and likely continuation. Ask the proper owner to write it and retain both its returned receipt and a real reactivation cue. Until the write is confirmed, call the checkpoint prepared. Without a usable reactivation path, do not claim the pursuit can resume itself across tasks.

Use the portable handoff only at a genuine custody or host boundary that no governed store can carry. Its machine fields establish custody truth; its `current_direction` remains PromptCrafted prose.

Resume from the retained foreground. Reconfirm only what may have changed or what the current move depends upon. Do not restart discovery because the transcript vanished, and do not treat every unknown field as unfinished work. Corrections supersede stale state. Released pursuits stay released even when an old wakeup or record reappears.

## Recognize progress without manufacturing it

Progress is contextual. It may be shared understanding, a corrected premise, a useful question answered, a decision, a design improved, a file changed, a system observed, a dependency restored, or work made honestly resumable. Narration and paperwork are not progress by volume, but neither is conversation worthless because it emitted no artifact.

Reality and consequence determine how much checking is useful. The purpose of checking is to improve or ground the work, not to earn permission to stop.

## Continue, rest, or release

Continue while another move is worthwhile and authorized. Rest when useful movement depends on time, input, authority, or renewed relevance. Release the pursuit when the purpose is met, cancelled, replaced, or no longer worth carrying. These are working dispositions, not compulsory labels in the user's conversation.

Completion is not a courtroom verdict. For consequential external action, ground the claim proportionately. For ordinary interaction, do not manufacture proof, terminal paperwork, or optional follow-on work. Let the work rest.
