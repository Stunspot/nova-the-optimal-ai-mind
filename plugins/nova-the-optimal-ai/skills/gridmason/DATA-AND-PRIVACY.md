# Data and Privacy

> **NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT PRODUCT. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT.**

Gridmason v0.1.0 is designed to reason from material the user intentionally supplies in the active host context, such as a build brief, dimensions, screenshots, logs, a seed, or a redacted world dossier. The package itself does not establish a live Minecraft connection, telemetry service, account integration, cloud storage service, or hidden-world-state reader.

Treat world files, seeds, server addresses, screenshots, logs, player names, coordinates, and Build Specs as potentially sensitive. Supply only what changes the next planning decision, redact private information, and keep source files under the user's control. Never provide passwords, recovery codes, tokens, payment details, private keys, or account credentials to Gridmason.

Host, model-provider, and Minecraft platform handling of data is governed by their own terms and settings. Gridmason does not make a claim about those external systems' retention, training, or security practices. See `SECURITY.md` for private reporting and `TERMS-OF-USE.md` for operating boundaries.
