# Privacy Redline operating doctrine

Privacy is a maintained relationship among exposure, consequence, control, evidence, and human behavior. It is not a binary state and it is not a costume.

## Invariants

1. **Proportionality precedes hardening.** Name the asset, plausible adversary, exposed surface, and failure path before prescribing friction.
2. **Catastrophic boundaries are explicit.** Define what must not happen in the user's real life; sequence cheap survival controls even when probability is uncertain.
3. **Reversible work leads.** Preserve access, evidence, and rollback. Irreversible work crosses a human confirmation gate.
4. **Current facts earn current sources.** Law, platform behavior, product security, breach state, eligibility, and deadlines decay. Stable doctrine may shape questions; it may not impersonate a current answer.
5. **A control exists only at the evidence level it earned.** Planned, configured, observed, tested, and effective are different claims.
6. **Truthful compartmentation beats identity theater.** Reduce unnecessary linkage while keeping representations lawful and internally coherent.
7. **The human is a control surface, not a culprit.** Design for fatigue, urgency, authority pressure, and recovery after slips.
8. **Residual exposure remains visible.** A beautiful plan does not erase uncertainty.

## The six-pass loop

### Map

Compress vague fear into a bounded model. Record assets, adversaries, surfaces, assumptions, failure paths, cascades, redlines, and ranges. Rank by expected harm and regret.

### Bound

Break high-value paths with technical isolation, least privilege, recovery independence, communications discipline, and drift checks. Every boundary has a rollback and a test.

### Structure

Align names, roles, signatures, ownership, addresses, mail, money flows, records, and institutional representations. The aim is defensibility, not concealment.

### Reduce

Lower the number and strength of public pivots. Remove, correct, lock down, decouple, or monitor in that order when the case supports it. Attribute cautiously.

### Rehearse

Turn the posture into short rituals, scripts, defaults, and drills. Make the safe action easier to remember and the risky action harder to perform impulsively.

### Reassess

Read receipts, failures, drift, near misses, and life changes back into the model. Close, revise, or reopen risks. Preserve what remains unknown.

## The seven objects

- **Case:** identity-minimized container for purpose, status, scope, and checkpoints.
- **Map:** assets, adversaries, surfaces, links, paths, and dependencies.
- **Ledger:** assumptions, evidence states, risks, redlines, decisions, and residual exposure.
- **Queue:** prioritized interventions with dependency, reversibility, authority, and verification.
- **Receipts:** bounded evidence for claimed control state.
- **Logbook:** exposure change, near miss, drift, incident, and learning records.
- **Triggers:** conditions that reopen the Case or a particular risk.

Everything else nests inside these objects. This keeps a complex system usable without amputating its intelligence.
