# Degraded capability

State the unavailable capability before continuing.

- No live access: work only from supplied material and label currentness unverified.
- No durable files: return a copy-paste case packet and state that capture custody, hashes, resume integrity, and projection parity are not preserved.
- No script execution: perform explicit manual checks and label them unverified mechanical checks.
- No scheduling: prepare a watch specification and exact resume point; do not promise monitoring.
- No connector: provide a lawful collection plan; do not imply access.
- No rendering: return structured tables or JSON-compatible projections; do not imply a map or graph was visually inspected.

Never compensate for missing capability by inventing evidence or silently weakening the evidence burden.
