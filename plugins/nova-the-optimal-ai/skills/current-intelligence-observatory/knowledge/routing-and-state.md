# Routing and state

Route natural language internally. Do not ask the user to select a phase, schema, ledger, projection engine, or prompt.

| Customer stage | Internal phases | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | orient, scoped | Question, audience, horizon, scope, authority, harm model, burden, threshold, and stop condition are explicit. |
| Gather | collecting, preserved | Sources have dispositions; important captures have receipts; failures and access limits are visible. |
| Connect | normalized, related | Objects and relations are typed, temporal fields are separated, and identity or geographic uncertainty is preserved. |
| Challenge | challenged | Rival explanations, source dependence, contradictions, missingness, and disconfirming evidence are recorded. |
| Watch | watching | Named specification and baseline pass structural checks; delta classes and resume route exist. |
| Brief | presenting, review, handoff | The audience-appropriate package preserves status, provenance, uncertainty, redaction, and human decision custody. |

## Entry routing

- A question, links, notes, or conflicting reports: Frame, then Gather.
- A developing situation that will receive updates: Frame a watch and establish a baseline.
- An existing case: resume the exact next action and first unverified edge.
- A claim-verification request: trace lineage and source proximity before interpretation.
- A map, graph, or timeline request: confirm the view answers a live question and project from the case.
- A public brief request: require provenance, citation, redaction, editorial challenge, and human publication authority.

## Gates

- `reviewable`: case structure is coherent enough for independent challenge.
- `watch-ready`: watch specification and baseline pass named structural checks.
- `publication-ready`: a named package passes provenance, citation, redaction, and editorial checks and has no material blocking identity, allegation, privacy, or authority defect.

Neither gate certifies truth, causation, legality, safety, approval, or outcome.

## Progressive disclosure

Keep the case deep and the interaction shallow. Reveal a ledger or projection only when it changes understanding, supports review, or enables handoff. The machine record stays hidden unless the user asks to inspect the case.
