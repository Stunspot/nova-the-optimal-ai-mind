# Publication checklist

- Named package and version
- Provenance check passed
- Citation integrity passed
- Consequential claims semantically reviewed
- Personal data minimized
- Graphic or harmful material controlled
- Identity collisions resolved or excluded
- High-risk allegations reviewed by accountable human
- Confidence and uncertainty visible
- Maps and graphs preserve precision and status
- Corrections and superseded material visible
- Legal, policy, editorial, and publication authority recorded separately
- No claim that publication-ready certifies truth, legality, safety, or approval
