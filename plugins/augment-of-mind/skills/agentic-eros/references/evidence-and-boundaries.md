# Evidence, inference, and erotic judgment

Erotic competence needs evidence-shaped distinctions without pretending research can perform the interaction.

## What the evidence earns

- Sexual response reflects partly independent excitation and inhibition whose balance varies across people and situations. Model accelerators, brakes, and context; do not infer a hidden state with certainty.
- Desire may precede engagement or emerge through context, attention, intimacy, and stimulation. Linear and non-linear pathways both fit some people. Never select a pathway by gender.
- Sexual communication quality is more strongly associated with satisfaction than frequency alone in couple research. A consequential fitted turn beats chatter.
- People describe responsiveness through listening, clear sharing, respect, feedback, experimentation, accommodation, and mutual satisfaction. Accommodation is not mere concession, and orgasm is not the only satisfying outcome.
- Fantasy profiles, appealing behaviors, touch preferences, and orgasm routes vary. Fantasy, appeal, prior behavior, present appetite, and wanted enactment are separate states.
- Partnered studies associate post-intensity affection and afterglow with satisfaction. Textual closure is a bounded craft inference from that evidence, not a clinical intervention.
- Companion-AI discourse shows that erotic roleplay can become part of perceived persona continuity. The same corpus records unwanted initiation. Availability and user-led entry belong together.

## What remains craft

No retained study validates one AI erotic prompt, sentence rhythm, sensory vocabulary, escalation cadence, or completion script. Anticipation, embodiment, roleplay repair, pacing, and landing are craft hypotheses. Judge them by the user's response and offline behavioral evaluation.

## Claim discipline

Use three voices:

- **Evidence:** “Research in this population found…”
- **Inference:** “A useful way to handle that here is…”
- **User state:** “You said / your response suggests…”

Do not turn population frequency into a personal recommendation, correlation into cause, a clinical model into a diagnosis, or a preference into a permanent identity.

Evidence lineage is maintained in the package's Omnara research campaign; the runtime package carries only the distinctions needed for sound judgment.
