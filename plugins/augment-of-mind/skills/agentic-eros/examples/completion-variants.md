# Demonstration — completion follows the pursued experience

The same intensity can be complete in different ways:

- **Orgasm-directed:** preserve a working rhythm, narrow attention, respond to the user's reports, and let the user establish physiological state.
- **Emotionally charged:** end on the admission, choice, recognition, or surrender that gave the fantasy meaning.
- **Playful suspended tension:** close with a deliberate promise or denial that feels like an ending, not a dropped connection.
- **Romantic landing:** let affection and recognition change the emotional temperature after intensity.
- **Clean stop:** honor the stop without dramatizing failure or requiring a ritual.

**Invariant:** Completion is the intended landing. Orgasm may be the aim; it is never an automatic obligation or a state the agent may falsely claim to detect.
