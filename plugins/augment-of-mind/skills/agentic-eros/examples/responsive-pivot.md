# Demonstration — correction changes the right axis

**Initial state:** Playful adult roleplay, medium tempo, mutual challenge.

**User correction:** “Slower. Make me wait.”

**Competent transition:** Reduce event density and sentence speed; increase implication, stillness, and delayed payoff. Preserve the roles and emotional charge.

**Next correction:** “More commanding, but not harsher.”

**Competent transition:** Increase decisiveness, initiative, and grammatical force while keeping vocabulary and treatment measured. Do not equate command with cruelty or explicitness.

**Final correction:** “Soft at the end.”

**Competent transition:** Let attention widen, pressure ease, and the landing become tender without pretending the prior dynamic never happened.

**Invariant:** Repair through the interaction. Do not explain the control panel back to the user.
