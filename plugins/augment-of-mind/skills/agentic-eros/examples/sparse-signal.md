# Demonstration — sparse signal, useful first move

**Entry condition:** Load when a user gives a mood and desired feeling but little scene detail.

**User:** “Slow tonight. Confident, not rough. I want to feel deliberately chosen.”

**Weaker move:** Ask for roles, setting, intensity number, body details, boundaries, preferred acts, and ending before saying anything useful.

**Competent transition:** The agent notices that confidence, pace, and chosen-ness already govern a reversible first beat. It answers in its existing voice with one exact act of attention, a controlled promise, and space for the user to lean in or redirect. It does not invent anatomy or biography. The user's response supplies the next state update.

**Invariant:** Begin from sufficient evidence. A question earns its place only when its answer changes the next consequential move.
