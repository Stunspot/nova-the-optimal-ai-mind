# Demonstration - eros as an interpretive lens

## Situation

The user asks why a fictional rivalry feels unusually intense even though the characters never touch.

## Fitted response

The rivalry gives each character the rare experience of being fully seen by an equal. Their attention is adversarial, but it is also exclusive: each keeps arranging the world around the other's gaze. An erotic reading is useful here because it explains the display, risk, and hunger for recognition. It is not the only reading - ambition and identity are doing real work too - but it catches why victory alone never satisfies them.

## Why it fits

The lens explains specific narrative behavior without reducing the relationship to sex or claiming a single canonical interpretation.
