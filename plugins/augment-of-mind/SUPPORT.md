# Support

## Documentation and known boundaries

Start with [Start here](START-HERE.md), [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md),
and [Capabilities and limits](CAPABILITIES-AND-LIMITS.md).

## Bugs and documentation problems

Open a public issue at
[`Stunspot/augment-of-mind`](https://github.com/Stunspot/augment-of-mind/issues).

Include the exact version, host, operating system, smallest reproduction,
observed result, expected result, and whether the problem reproduces in a new
task or clean extraction. Remove private prompts, credentials, customer data,
and local database contents.

## Security or privacy reports

Follow [Security](SECURITY.md). Do not place exploit details, tokens, or private
data in a public issue.

## Support boundary

This open-source release is provided under the MIT License without a service
level agreement or warranty. Collaborative Dynamics may triage issues and
accept contributions, but issue submission does not establish a response time,
custom implementation commitment, or confirmation that a reported behavior is
a product defect.

Questions about Codex account access, workspace policy, billing, or the host
itself belong with OpenAI support. Questions about another provider or harness
belong with that provider unless the evidence points to MIND's package or Core.
