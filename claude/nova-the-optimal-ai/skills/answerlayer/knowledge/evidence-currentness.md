# Evidence that supports this answer

Inspect the source before using it as support. Record its title, locator, publication date, access time and status, then map the relevant claim to a passage/table/section and its scope. Label the relation supports, contradicts or context. Quote only what is necessary and permitted; a precise paraphrase plus location can be enough. Track the original source through `origin_id`: repeated coverage of one announcement is one lineage, not independent corroboration.

Authority is claim-specific. A vendor is primary about its published limit but interested about comparative superiority. An official notice establishes the text it issues, not every downstream causal forecast. Report inaccessible, supplied-but-unexamined, incomplete or conflicted evidence accurately. A citation manifest inventories what was supplied; it does not fetch, inspect or verify it.

## Currentness and uncertainty

Tie every current claim to a cutoff, applicable population/product/jurisdiction and the relevant dates. Unknown dates remain unknown. A future-effective development can support present preparation while remaining future-effective. Historical Almanac originals are examples of method and past claims; verify live facts independently with current primary material before issuing current guidance.

Assign uncertainty to the proposition it affects. Distinguish uncertainty about the source, observation, applicability, causal mechanism and prediction. Seek independent evidence only where it can materially change the decision. When sources conflict, first compare time, scope, definitions and authority; retain genuine unresolved contradictions. Explain what observation would distinguish them and how waiting affects the decision.

The native adoption helper requires known current/result cutoffs, dated referenced sources no later than the result cutoff, declared inspected/supplied support and at least one support relation. These are guardrails around supplied records. They do not establish that a passage was actually read, that it entails the claim, or that the recommendation is wise. Synthetic fixture support is expressly fictional and cannot be reused as real evidence.
