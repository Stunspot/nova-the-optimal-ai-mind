# Review the deliverable family, not the story around it

Review only the claim actually being made: a single deliverable can be fit for a named handoff without proving the whole Officefile ready. Bind the verdict to the supplied revision, artifacts, observations, and intended next use.

## The integrity claims

1. **Custody and meaning.** Consequential facts, names, dates, units, recommendations, template authority, and source status remain traceable. Conflicts and inference do not masquerade as verified truth.
2. **Family coherence.** Where several artifacts claim to describe one decision, their consequential facts, terminology, and source/derivative relationship agree. A derivative is regenerated from its authoritative source rather than patched into a competing truth.
3. **Earned quality claims.** File validity, formulas, visual layout, reading order, contrast, links, metadata, privacy, editability, and export fidelity are represented only by observations actually supplied. A container listing, render log, manual check, and native-app observation are different evidence classes.
4. **Useable handoff.** The recipient can identify each artifact, format, editability, canonical source, check status, unresolved matter, approval owner, and next action. Produced, inspected, reviewed, and approved remain distinct states.
5. **Authority and recovery.** The packet keeps user subject truth, template/brand authority, privacy choices, approval, publication, upload, and overwrite with their accountable owner. It can resume from stated sources and decisions without silently widening authority.

## Disposition discipline

- **PASS:** all material integrity claims for the named next use are supported by reviewed evidence. Record any improvements separately.
- **PASS_WITH_CONDITIONS:** the next use remains bounded and responsible if explicit, independently checkable conditions close before it. Conditions cannot paper over a material observed defect or an unknown that defeats the claim.
- **REVISE:** a reviewed artifact, record, or seam contains a material defect. Return the smallest upstream cause and its closure condition.
- **BLOCKED:** the reviewer lacks decisive scope, evidence, criterion, or authority to make the requested judgment. Name the missing item and the narrowest re-entry condition.

Reopen the affected judgment when the factual spine, artifact/source relationship, evidence basis, accessibility/privacy posture, approval scope, or requested use materially changes. Editorial changes that cannot affect those claims do not reopen the whole review.
