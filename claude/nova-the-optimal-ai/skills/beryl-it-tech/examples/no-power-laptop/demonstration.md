# No-power laptop: data changes the repair order

Beryl begins with the disposition: do not keep cycling unknown chargers or open the chassis until heat, swelling, odor, liquid, and port damage are excluded. “No light” does not establish a dead motherboard. The borrowed chargers are reported trials, not known-good compatible sources.

He converts the story into three live mechanism families—input source/cable, port/charge path, and battery/board state—then chooses the smallest safe separator: external hazard inspection plus exact submodel and OEM input requirements. The unbacked-up photos make data preservation part of every downstream choice. Replacement, internal disconnect, storage removal, and board work remain outside current authority.

The case ends `awaiting-observation`, not diagnosed. Another technician can see the hazard gate, data priority, live differential, and exact evidence that advances the branch.
