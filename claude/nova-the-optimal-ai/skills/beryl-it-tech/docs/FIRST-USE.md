# Catch what changes while the problem happens

Open Beryl Repair Companion when you want a small observation of this Windows PC, a comparison with another moment, or a readable investigation Beryl has prepared. You can begin without creating a case or filling in a record.

The companion keeps observations and selected originals on this computer. Beryl does the interpretation in your existing conversation. A copied request waits for you to paste it there.

## Take your first observation

Start from the Beryl Repair Companion shortcut after installation. On **Start & return**, choose **Check this Windows PC**. You will see the exact scope before anything runs: CPU time over three seconds, physical RAM availability, space on the Windows system volume, and power state.

Choose **Run 3-second observation** when the moment is useful to your question. Keep the action that was slow and the conditions around it in your conversation with Beryl; the sample does not record your workload. When the operation finishes, the status says **Observation saved** and a dated sample appears with the observation results.

Read the values and any unavailable-field reason. CPU covers the stated interval; RAM, space and power are read at its end. A missing measure remains unavailable. It does not mean zero use or a healthy device. Open **Measurement scope and machine identity** for the exact saved reading and its limits.

To stop an observation before it finishes, choose **Cancel observation** and wait for the operation to stop. A cancelled observation is not saved. If a prepared investigation tells you to stop work or refer the device, follow that instruction before taking another observation.

## Compare two useful moments

Take a second observation when it helps distinguish the situation, then choose **Compare samples**. Select two different dated readings using **First selection** and **Second selection**. The display retains both original values and shows second minus first. The selections need not be chronological; use their dates to keep the comparison clear.

Choose **Copy comparison for Beryl**, paste the text into your existing Beryl conversation, and describe what you were trying to do at each moment. The companion confirms the copy; it does not send a message. If clipboard access is unavailable, it opens the same text for you to select and copy.

This gives Beryl bounded facts and a concrete question. It does not identify a cause or establish that a repair worked. [Understand the observation limits](OBSERVATION-LIMITS.md) before drawing conclusions from a change.

## Start with a log or photograph instead

Choose **Select a log or screenshot** on Start & return, or **Select existing material** in **Sources & Beryl**. Select an ordinary file that belongs to the problem. Its exact original is retained; an ordinary file remains source-only until Beryl prepares an interpretation.

Open the filename to inspect its preview or image. **Save a copy of the exact original** retrieves the retained bytes. A preview can be incomplete; Beryl can read the retained original through the local route.

In **Sources & Beryl**, choose **Copy local request for Beryl** and paste it into your existing conversation. The request supplies the actual saved folder and local bridge. Ask Beryl to inspect the material and prepare a supported investigation. You do not need to author a case file. After Beryl has published the result locally, choose **Refresh prepared work** and open it in **Investigations**.

[Read a prepared investigation](READ-AN-INVESTIGATION.md) to follow the evidence, rival explanations and unresolved next step. The three fictional examples on Start & return are separate demonstrations; their contents are not observations of your device.

## Finish and return

Choose **Exit** when no operation is running. Wait for **Beryl is closed** and **Local service stopped**. Reopen the same shortcut to return to your saved selection and environment.

For reconnecting, unavailable material or moving a saved folder, use [Return and recovery](RETURN-AND-RECOVERY.md).
