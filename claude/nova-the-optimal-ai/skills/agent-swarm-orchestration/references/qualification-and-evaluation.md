# Qualify mechanisms before turning them into doctrine

Agent orchestration evidence is task-, model-, prompt-, tool-, budget-, and runtime-sensitive. Treat research and local runs as priors with boundaries. Do not turn a benchmark win, a vivid demo, or one successful swarm into a universal routing law.

## Label instruction strength

Use these labels when a distinction matters:

- **Invariant:** required for authority, safety, evidence, state integrity, or responsible closure.
- **Default:** the normal route because current evidence and operational cost favor it; exceptions need a reason.
- **Option:** a legitimate design choice whose fit is contextual.
- **Experiment:** promising but not qualified for default use; isolate and cap it.
- **Example:** a provocation or illustration, never a prescribed category.

Root mission custody, non-expanding authority, source-bound dependencies, single-writer canonical state, honest evidence levels, bounded interaction, and complete responsibility disposition are invariants. Headcount, cast, pattern recipe, relation vocabulary, exposure timing, root-owned spawning, model mixture, round count, resolver, pruning threshold, and dynamic recruitment are defeasible.

## Compare mechanisms, not mythology

For a consequential new recipe or default, compare prospectively with:

1. a strong Direct route;
2. the nearest simpler workflow;
3. an equal-budget independent-sampling route where relevant;
4. the proposed organization under the same acceptance boundary.

Ablate the alleged cause. Remove or shuffle persona labels while retaining function. Hold labels constant while changing evidence, tools, or transformations. Vary blind-first, selective exposure, and all-to-all sharing. Change the resolver without changing communication. Compare fixed and adaptive membership. Keep budgets and oracles matched.

Measure task-native outcome, corrected and introduced errors, unique evidence contribution, minority-correction survival, handoff loss, source use, context loss, tokens, wall time, recovery, write collisions, authority violations, privacy exposure, and security contamination.

## Distinguish qualification layers

- **Prompt decision cases** show whether the skill selects and describes an organization appropriately in isolated context.
- **Live-control cases** show whether real spawning, messages, handoffs, selective exposure, mutation, transitions, and closure followed the contract.
- **End-state oracles** show whether the task result is correct or fit for purpose at the exercised boundary.
- **Repeated matched trials** estimate whether a mechanism adds value reliably enough to promote.
- **Independent review** challenges contamination, hidden oracle leakage, overclaiming, and evidence custody.

A structural plan validator cannot establish synergy. A semantic reviewer cannot establish a filesystem post-state. A successful live trace cannot establish universal task advantage. Keep each claim inside its evidence layer.

## Promote and retire deliberately

Promote an Experiment to Option or Default only when repeated prospective evidence shows task-relevant benefit over appropriate baselines without an indispensable safety failure. Name the qualifying task class, model/runtime boundary, budget, and evidence cutoff.

Demote or retire a default when benefit disappears on stronger baselines, model capability changes, coordination cost rises, contamination or authority failures emerge, or users repeatedly need to fight the pattern. Preserve the historical evidence and the reason for the change.

## Current research priors

The 2026-08-31 OMNARA campaign found no support for swarm-by-default, sacred headcount, fixed topology, mandatory debate, or persona names as competence. It found that decomposability, baseline strength, real diversity of evidence or transformation, selective information exposure, explicit resolution, verification, and bounded adaptation materially affect outcomes.

The strongest matched study in that campaign reported no average multi-agent gain across all evaluated configurations, with large positive and negative task-dependent swings. Other studies found coordination collapse with scale, persona inconsistency, and cost growth, alongside narrower evidence for author-review-meta-review, blackboard-style discovery, dynamic pruning, and direct-versus-multi cascades. These are research signals, not production guarantees.

Dynamic recruitment, secure cross-session evidence lineage, correlated-error measurement, rollback after side effects, and long-horizon organizational morphogenesis remain open. Keep them Experimental until live prospective evidence earns more.

## Use the vNext catalogs

The maintained source repository keeps versioned vNext eval catalogs outside the distributable skill tree; they are developer qualification materials, not runtime dependencies. The natural cases probe anti-rigidity, role causality, example forms, resolver integrity, exposure, transitions, missing-source safety, adversarial contributions, invented organizations, and closure. Live cases require event traces and pre/post state. Ablations distinguish functional value from lanyards.

Those catalogs are acceptance specifications until executed. Never cite their existence as a pass.
