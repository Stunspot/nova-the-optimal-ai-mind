# Pattern recipes are provocations, not a router

Each recipe offers a causal mechanism, a characteristic failure, adaptation knobs, and a nearest simpler baseline. Use it as a starting sketch. Combine recipes across phases or invent another composition when none expresses the task's needs.

## Direct then escalate

**Use when:** a strong direct route often succeeds, failure is observable, or the task is initially too uncertain to justify a team.

**Mechanism:** root or one qualified route attempts the task; a verifier checks named criteria; further contributions enter only for failed criteria or unresolved uncertainty.

**Failure mode:** the direct attempt anchors every later worker, or escalation becomes a ritual after success.

**Adaptation knobs:** hide the direct answer from independent challengers, recruit only the missing evidence or transformation, change model/tool for the failed edge, stop after verified acceptance.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** Direct without escalation.

## Fan-out and merge

**Use when:** acquisition or analysis partitions cleanly and returns share a consumable schema.

**Mechanism:** independent shards inspect non-overlapping loci; schema checks normalize returns; root performs union, deduplication, comparison, and synthesis.

**Failure mode:** hidden dependencies, duplicated reads, incompatible definitions, or overlapping writes.

**Adaptation knobs:** repartition by evidence locality or consumer, add a missing shard, collapse unproductive shards, or introduce one integrator after blind returns.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** Enlist one representative shard, then expand only if coverage value warrants it.

## Specialist relay or bucketline

**Use when:** each transformation genuinely depends on the accepted output of the prior stage.

**Mechanism:** typed handoffs, per-stage acceptance, provenance, bottleneck visibility, and repair from the first unearned edge.

**Failure mode:** fake parallelism creates stale downstream work, or a single failure restarts the entire line.

**Adaptation knobs:** merge stages that add handoff loss, split a bottleneck, add a verifier at a risky boundary, or Recover one stage with a changed tool or representation.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** root performs the short chain directly.

## Independent jury

**Use when:** the answer space is discrete, genuine independence is possible, and a suitable adjudicator or objective check exists.

**Mechanism:** blind proposals or judgments, optional targeted critique, then objective adjudication, evidence-weighted selection, or a justified vote.

**Failure mode:** shared framing makes votes correlated, majority suppresses a correct minority, or confidence replaces evidence.

**Adaptation knobs:** diversify evidence or tools, preserve minority reports, remove cross-talk, change the resolver, or ask for a discriminating test.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** strong Direct plus one independent verifier.

## Author, review, meta-review, revise

**Use when:** a draft benefits from independent defect discovery and a bounded correction cycle.

**Mechanism:** author creates; reviewers inspect independently; a meta-review or root resolves duplicates and conflicts into one feedback packet; one writer revises; a verifier checks the claimed improvement.

**Failure mode:** reviewers see the intended verdict, all reviewers repeat the same lens, or several writers collide on the artifact.

**Adaptation knobs:** specialize reviews by failure class, preserve blind first passes, limit cross-response, change the integrator, cap revision rounds.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** one author plus one independent review.

## Evidence blackboard or anthill

**Use when:** information is distributed, private, or local to tools and workers can respond to a well-scoped need.

**Mechanism:** broadcast the evidence need; local contributors inspect only their partition; candidate responses remain private from peers; a request-scoped board records provenance-bearing evidence marks; root or curator reconciles.

**Failure mode:** the board becomes untrusted canonical truth, exposes private data, permits peer overwrite, or creates a permanent helper population.

**Adaptation knobs:** narrow the need, add or release data-local contributors, require provenance, quarantine adversarial entries, expire the board after resolution.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** Enlist the most likely evidence locus before broadcasting wider.

## Mission-command unit or platoon

**Use when:** an accepted intent can be decomposed into bounded local execution units with real ownership and separately observable state.

**Mechanism:** root expresses intent and constraints; unit owners act locally inside narrowed authority; shared surfaces have one writer; situation reports expose state; an independent verifier checks authoritative results.

**Failure mode:** hierarchy theater substitutes for interfaces, subordinates invent authority, or a leader's report is treated as post-state evidence.

**Adaptation knobs:** change unit boundaries, centralize a shared artifact, add a reserve or verifier, release completed units, transition to a chain for integration.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** one writer implements while one reviewer inspects.

## Deliberative council or boardroom

**Use when:** several warranted viewpoints, evidence sets, consequences, or value-bearing criteria must be made mutually legible before a decision.

**Mechanism:** charter contributions by causal standing; prepare independently; expose selected claims for challenge; preserve conflicts and minority evidence; use a named chair, resolver, or human decision right.

**Failure mode:** fixed-persona theater, endless all-to-all debate, consensus laundering, or invented representation of stakeholders.

**Adaptation knobs:** change the cast by contribution need, introduce outside evidence, narrow a cross-response, separate factual adjudication from value decision, stop when the decision object is complete.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** root compares independent memos without a discussion round.

## Advisory staff or general staff

**Use when:** functionally distinct specialist products must support one accountable decision owner.

**Mechanism:** intelligence, operations, logistics, risk, legal, technical, or other functions prepare products against a shared planning frame; an integrator reconciles premises; the decision owner decides.

**Failure mode:** advice becomes line command, staff consensus widens authority, or specialist silos deliver incompatible products.

**Adaptation knobs:** change functions, standardize assumptions, add a red-team estimate, ask one staff function to translate for another, return reserved choices to the owner.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** Enlist one generalist analyst and retain the decision at root.

## Planner, executor, verifier

**Use when:** tool-mediated work has separable plan, action, and observable state checks.

**Mechanism:** plan under current evidence; execute only authorized bounded actions; independently inspect resulting state; repair or stop from observed failure.

**Failure mode:** planner assumes capabilities, executor expands authority, verifier reviews prose rather than state, or failed verification triggers a blind rerun.

**Adaptation knobs:** change the plan representation, narrow the action, replace the tool route, strengthen post-state observation, or escalate a reserved action.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** root plans, acts, and performs a direct state check.

## Dynamic coalition or complex adaptive hybrid

**Use when:** contribution needs cannot be known fully upfront, the environment supplies usable feedback, and adaptation can be bounded and observed.

**Mechanism:** begin with the least speculative adequate composition; measure contribution and acceptance progress; recruit or prune on named signals; change relations, exposure, resolver, or regime by phase; retain an acyclic event and evidence history.

**Failure mode:** agent inflation, confidence-driven rewiring, topology churn, lost state, or endless emergence with no acceptance owner.

**Adaptation knobs:** hard round and cost caps, minimum evidence for recruitment, contribution telemetry, hysteresis before reversal, phase gates, forced simplification after failed adaptation.

**Nearest simpler baseline:** the nearest fixed organization under the same budget.

## Inventing a new composition

When no recipe fits, begin with the missing cognition rather than a metaphor. Name the contributions; explain why each can alter the result; connect them through execution, exposure, influence, and authority edges; choose the resolver and verifier; partition state; name reform signals; compile only the next phase.

If an analogy helps, state what mechanics it contributes and what implications are rejected. A useful new pattern is allowed to look nothing like this library. Its burden is causal coherence, bounded operation, and accountable closure—not admission to the zoo.
