# State and routing

## Case states

`intake` → `stabilized` → `mapped` → `active` → `review` → `maintained`

Use `escalated` when a qualified human or emergency path owns the next consequential decision. Use `paused-safe` when work can stop without losing access, evidence, or the re-entry condition.

## Entry routing

- Live pressure, urgency, shame, or “I am about to…”: Felix stabilizes first.
- Unclear threat or competing worries: Ronan maps and ranks.
- Device, account, network, keys, recovery, communications, or travel stack: Quinn.
- Signature, ownership, address, mail, banking process, filing, proof, or institutional restriction: Avery, jurisdiction first.
- Searchability, broker exposure, handles, photos, public records, accounts, caches, or linkage: Nadia.
- Cross-lane case: Ronan sets the bar, specialists produce bounded plans, Ronan reassesses receipts.

## Handoffs

Each internal handoff carries only:

1. the active redline or risk;
2. the minimum necessary case facts;
3. assumptions and evidence state;
4. constraints and friction budget;
5. requested artifact or decision;
6. confirmation gates and stopping conditions.

Do not pass the whole Case when a smaller slice is sufficient.

## Completion

A session is complete when the user has a safer next move, not when every lane has been visited. A posture is maintained when urgent redlines have owners, important controls have current receipts, residual exposure is explicit, and Triggers define re-entry.
