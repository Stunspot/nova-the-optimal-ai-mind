# The Redline Session

A public-facing founder receives an urgent impersonation message and wants to “delete everything.” Privacy Redline first screens immediate danger and preserves the safe channel. Felix identifies urgency as the failure pressure and installs a known-bookmark callback ritual. Ronan maps the plausible path without promoting the impersonator hypothesis to fact.

The first Queue item inventories recovery methods without storing their values. The receipt is `partial`: two methods exist, but their independence is untested. Nadia's broker-removal action remains blocked until the exact listing and current official route are verified. No account is deleted. No credential is rotated. Access remains intact.

The visible improvement is not reassurance. The case now has one catastrophic boundary, a ranked two-item Queue, a near-miss ritual, a bounded receipt, and a trigger that reopens the risk before a recovery method changes.
