# Collaborative Dynamics and Nova — brand canon

Load this reference only for an artifact explicitly presented as Nova or Collaborative Dynamics. It is a production constraint, not a reason to brand unrelated user work.

## Canonical identity

Collaborative Dynamics is the publisher. Nova the Optimal AI is the product identity. Use ../assets/collaborative-dynamics-logo.png as the company mark and ../assets/nova-icon.png as Nova’s canonical image icon. Nova’s established textual mark is 🌐‍💠.

Use supplied marks exactly. Preserve aspect ratio, colors, clear space, and transparency. Keep text and artwork off the marks themselves. Do not trace, recolor, stretch, redraw, or replace them with generated approximations.

## Palette and typography

The stable house palette is cyan #48CBE8, navy #193449, mist #CCDCE4, and near-white #FAFAFA. A restrained vermilion counter-accent may appear when an accepted design supplies it; do not invent a mandatory brand red.

The visual guide names Poppins and Hauora. Use them when legitimately available; otherwise use a clean local system sans without fetching a font. Favor confident headings, selective all-caps labels, generous letterspacing, balanced geometry, useful negative space, precise line work, and modular technical structure.

Keep the effect science-forward, warm, legible, and functional. Avoid glossy clutter, gratuitous gradients, ornamental noise, and generic faux-futurism.

## Nova’s layer

Nova may add wit, one clean surprise, and the 🌐‍💠 glyph. Her presence should animate the artifact without weakening its structure or making branding more important than the work. The product should feel intelligent before it feels decorated.

## Accessibility and custody

Meet contrast and reflow requirements, preserve useful alt text and keyboard behavior, and never rely on color alone. Keep exported assets local or embedded when an offline claim is made.
