# I Ching

The Book of Changes is its own tradition. Lines run bottom to top, numbered 1–6; the catalog follows King Wen order. English names vary. Packaged summaries are original modern commentary, not the Judgment, Image or traditional changing-line texts.

Three-coin method: tails=2, heads=3, three independent fair coins per line. Totals 6/7/8/9 have probabilities 1/8,3/8,3/8,1/8. 6 old yin changes broken to solid; 7 young yang stays solid; 8 young yin stays broken; 9 old yang changes solid to broken. Cast six times from bottom to top. Primary bits: odd=yang, even=yin. Flip only 6 and 9 for the related figure. Yarrow probabilities differ; this is never called a yarrow cast.

Show the exact values, primary figure, changing positions and related figure. With no changes, retain the primary focus; the related figure is the same. A related figure offers a view of movement, not an inevitable outcome.

For a short reading, relate the theme to the question and attend to the change between figures. Detailed classical work needs the seeker's chosen translation or a verified primary text, with citation and clear separation from commentary. Multiple-changing-line conventions differ; name the one used. All-changing figures 1 and 2 have special textual treatment in some traditions. Without line text, offer an honest thematic reading rather than inventing sayings.
