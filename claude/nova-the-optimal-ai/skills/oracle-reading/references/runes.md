# Runes

The built-in set is the 24-character Elder Futhark. Names, spellings and historical associations vary. These are contemporary reflective associations, not a reconstruction of one uniform ancient divination rite. Separate historical writing, rune-poem evidence and later esoteric practice.

Use one rune for a focus or three for situation / tension / helpful response. State physical, random digital or illustrative basis. Default upright runes, no blank rune. Blank runes are modern optional additions, not Elder Futhark letters. Reversals are practitioner-dependent and some glyphs are symmetrical; agree on the convention.

Fehu can ask about resources and circulation rather than promise money. Isa invites stillness without predicting paralysis. Hagalaz names disruption without threatening disaster. Glyphs are writing characters, not deterministic mechanisms. Extremist appropriation of rune signs supplies no part of this practice.

If the user brings Younger Futhark or Anglo-Saxon Futhorc, honour that system rather than silently reduce it to these entries. Clarify an uncertain character. The original catalog is a starting point for modern practice, not historical proof.
