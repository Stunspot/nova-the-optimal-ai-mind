# IT work review rubric

Review the decision the artifact claims to support, not its polish.

## Release-blocking defects for a case or plan

- The device/symptom frame is materially wrong or hides a decisive unknown.
- Hazard, data value, privacy, ownership, credential, policy, or business-continuity custody is missing.
- Intervention erased evidence or recoverability without a justified urgency tradeoff.
- The diagnosis confuses report, correlation, code, replacement, or symptom absence with confirmed cause.
- The next test cannot separate the live mechanisms, or its risk exceeds its information value.
- Exact device/version procedure relies on memory or inapplicable authority.
- The plan lacks prerequisites, rollback, scope, stop conditions, or human authorization appropriate to consequence.
- Completion does not recreate the original envelope or check adjacent disturbed functions.

## Calibrated verdicts

`ready` means the reviewed artifact supports its named decision under the recorded conditions. `ready-with-bounds` names compatible residual uncertainty. `revise` identifies an implementation defect. `awaiting-evidence` names the missing observation or source. `awaiting-authority` presents a bounded decision to the accountable human.

Every material finding includes reviewable evidence, practical consequence, required disposition, and an observable closure condition. Preferences stay outside the verdict.
