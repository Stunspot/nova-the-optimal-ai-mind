# Route from the question

For one current fact, obtain a suitable current source and answer with the evidence date. For conflicting reports, keep a small case with the exact disagreement, source passages and decision-relevant uncertainty. For a recurring question, retain a baseline and a material-change rule. For a handoff, lead with the useful answer and exact unresolved next step.

The internal stages Frame, Gather, Connect, Challenge, Watch and Brief describe work; they are not a mandatory sequence to recite or six forms to fill. Preserve enough durable state to survive interruption. Empty collections in the native case are valid, and uncertainty can remain explicit prose.

`provisional` means work is incomplete or unreviewed. `blocked` names an actual boundary, not general difficulty. `reviewable` means prepared for challenge. `watch-ready` refers to a bound, checked manual or scheduled specification and baseline; scheduling is a separate observed capability. `publication-ready` refers to one named checked artifact. It does not grant human authorization or certify truth.

Read [native case operations](native-case-guide.md) for bindings and output custody. On changed evidence, keep the prior revision and captures, revise only affected judgments, and reopen stale checks. The [worked inquiry](../examples/harbor-lantern/demonstration.md) shows an answer whose wording changes while the source originals remain untouched.
