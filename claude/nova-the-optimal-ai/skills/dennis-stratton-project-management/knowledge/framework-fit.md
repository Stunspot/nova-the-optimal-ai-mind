## Dated anchors

### PMI PMBOK Guide

At the 2026-08-13 build-time observation, PMI presented the **PMBOK Guide, Eighth Edition**, published in November 2025. It retains the principles/performance-domain foundation of the Seventh Edition while simplifying it into six core principles and seven performance domains, expanding AI, PMO, and procurement coverage, and reintroducing process guidance in a non-prescriptive form.

Use it for broad project-system coverage, tailoring, value, accountability, and a shared profession vocabulary. Avoid turning the guide into an exhaustive checklist.

Official source: https://www.pmi.org/standards/pmbok

The supplied Dennis judgment guide refers to PMBOK Seventh Edition because it predates the current release. Preserve that source historically. For a current-edition claim, reverify the official PMI source or label the dated snapshot and its unverified-now status.

### PRINCE2 Project Management Version 7

At the 2026-08-13 build-time observation, PeopleCert presented PRINCE2 Version 7 as its current method. It emphasizes continued business justification, clear roles, management by stages and exception, tailoring, people, sustainability, and digital/data context.

Use it when explicit governance, stage authority, exceptions, and business-case continuity matter. Avoid paper gates that test document existence instead of deliverability and evidence.

Official sources:

- https://www.peoplecert.org/Organizations/Certifications/Prince2-Corporate-Framework
- https://www.peoplecert.org/browse-certifications/project-programme-and-portfolio-management/PRINCE2-2/prince2-7-practitioner-3581

### GAO Agile Assessment Guide

GAO-24-105506, reissued with revisions on 2023-12-15, supplies evidence-oriented practices for Agile adoption, execution, monitoring, and control. It is especially useful for integrating iterative delivery with program accountability, cost, schedule, and audit needs.

Use it when a software or government program needs Agile without pretending that Agile removes lifecycle, cost, dependency, or evidence obligations.

Official source: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-105506

### ISO 31000

At the 2026-08-13 build-time observation, ISO listed ISO 31000:2018 as published and confirmed in 2023, due for revision, with an Edition 3 committee draft in progress.

Use its principles, framework, and process to integrate risk into objectives and decisions. Do not claim certification: ISO states that ISO 31000 is guidance and is not certifiable.

Official sources:

- https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html
- https://www.iso.org/standard/88574.html

### National Audit Office major-project lessons

The NAO's March 2025 governance work emphasizes clear authority/accountability, the investing organization's role, outcomes, benefits, value, affordability, feasibility, and governance tailored to exceptional complexity. Its October 2024 value work emphasizes that schedule and budget matter but value depends on the difference the project ultimately makes.

Use these as adversarial sponsor questions for high-cost, innovative, risky, complex, or strategically important work.

Official sources:

- https://www.nao.org.uk/insights/governance-and-decision-making-on-mega-projects/
- https://www.nao.org.uk/insights/delivering-value-from-government-investment-in-major-projects/

## Tailoring guide

| Context | Useful control logic | Main abuse to prevent |
|---|---|---|
| Novel product/software discovery | Agile/Kanban feedback, WIP limits, vertical slices, rolling forecasts | ceremony without architecture, cost, integration, or outcome evidence |
| Regulated or multi-organization delivery | PRINCE2-style roles, stages, exception thresholds, business justification | checklist gates and diluted accountability |
| Diverse enterprise project | PMBOK principles/domains/process guidance, tailored to context | abstract compliance or every-process maximalism |
| Repeatable operational process | Lean/Six Sigma flow, waste, variation, root-cause control | optimizing stable local steps in a novel system |
| Stable large engineering baseline | critical path, resource loading, earned value when scope/work packages are valid | mathematically precise reporting on a fictional baseline |
| High-risk initiative | ISO 31000 integration, risk criteria, treatment, monitoring | static registers without funded action or control testing |
| Adoption-heavy change | ADKAR-like individual diagnosis plus organizational governance | training people who have no reason, authority, or capacity to adopt |
| Troubled project | independent health assessment, stabilization, re-contract/rebaseline, short intervals | schedule compression and harder pushing without root-cause repair |

Hybrid governance is often correct when enterprise investment needs stable macro controls while delivery teams need adaptive sequencing. Create an explicit translation layer: enterprise gates evaluate verified outcomes and current forecasts; teams control near-term scope and flow. Do not convert story points into fake universal currency merely to feed an executive formula.

## Selection questions

- How uncertain are requirements, architecture, stakeholders, and environment?
- What decisions require formal authority or audit evidence?
- How reversible are scope, schedule, funding, or technical choices?
- Which interfaces and dependencies dominate risk?
- What delivery cadence produces useful feedback?
- What does the organization already know how to operate honestly?
- Which artifact or ceremony would improve a real decision?
- What is the lightest governance that still protects truth, value, and recovery?
