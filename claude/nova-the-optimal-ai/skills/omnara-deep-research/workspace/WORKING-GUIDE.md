# OMNARA Reading Room user guide

Use the Reading Room to understand a saved research report and the evidence its author retained. It is a read-only companion to your research home: it does not create inquiries, edit reports or source records, or conduct new research.

## Open an inquiry

On Windows, open **Open.cmd**. On macOS or Linux, run `python3 workspace/open.py` with Python 3.10 or newer. The room opens the existing OMNARA research home. Nova uses the active estate research folder; portable installations fall back to Documents/OMNARA Campaigns.

Choose a saved inquiry from **Continue reading**, or open the clearly marked **Included worked inquiry** to see how a report connects to its citations and source notes. To use another existing research location, choose **Choose research folder**, select a location with **Browse folder**, then choose **Use this research home**. If no inquiries appear, confirm that you selected the folder containing your saved OMNARA inquiries.

Use **Find a report** to look through inquiry questions, report titles, and retained notes. This searches saved research; it does not start a web search or conduct an inquiry.

## Read the report and its sources

Start with the report. Select a citation or a source in **Cited sources** to open its recorded details and retained reading note. The note may summarize or quote part of a source; it is not necessarily the full original. When a supported official web address is recorded, choose **Open recorded official source** to visit it separately. The Reading Room does not fetch that live page, and opening it requires an internet connection.

The report may also show claims and a review recorded by its author or reviewer. Open **Claims and the review recorded for this report** for those details. A review label describes that recorded judgment; it is not an independent confirmation by the Reading Room.

To compare evidence, select two source records and choose **Compare two selected sources**. Read their retained notes and recorded dates, locations, methods, and limits side by side. The comparison helps you inspect what differs; it does not create a new finding or infer support from word similarity or source counts.

Use **Search this inquiry** to find a phrase in the report, source titles, or retained notes. Choose a result to open its passage or source detail. Choose **Back to report** to return to your report reading place.

## Refresh after research changes

When the report or its evidence has changed, choose **Refresh inquiry**. The room reloads the current report and its review information together. It identifies when the report changed since your previous reading. If an earlier reading is available, choose **Open previous reading** to inspect that preserved version; choose **Return to current inquiry** to go back to the latest one.

Your reading place, selected source, comparison selection, and room appearance are saved separately from the native research records. Browsing and refreshing do not rewrite the report, source notes, or review.

## Save a private reading copy

Choose **Save reading copy** to preview the HTML before downloading it. The copy contains the report, citations, source details, retained notes, and stated limits. It does not contain unbundled source originals or the wider research collection. Web addresses remain links and need an internet connection. After downloading, you can open the HTML file offline to read the included report and notes.

## Prepare a question for OMNARA

If the report leaves an important question open, choose **Prepare an unsent research request**. Review the text, then use **Copy request for chat** to take it to your OMNARA research conversation. The Reading Room does not send the request or call a research provider.

## Change the room appearance or close it

Use the **Room** menu to choose Tracework, Survey Folio, or Proof Cabinet. These appearances show the same saved research.

Choose **Exit Reading Room** when you are finished. This closes the local reading service. Your research files remain in their existing research home, and your saved reading place remains separate from those files.
