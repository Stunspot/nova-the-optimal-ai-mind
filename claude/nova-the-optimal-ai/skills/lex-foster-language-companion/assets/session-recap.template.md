## Next retrieval cue

Describe a small changed situation that requires the same capability without copying the original answer.

A supplied model or planned exercise is not observed learner progress. For transfer, name the earlier observation and the meaningful changed cue for the same specific criterion and modality. Preserve contrary evidence; do not erase history. Skip the recap when the requested answer is already complete and no continuation is wanted.
