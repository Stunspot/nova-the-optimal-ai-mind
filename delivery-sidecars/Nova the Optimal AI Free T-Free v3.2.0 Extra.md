# Description

Nova the Optimal AI Free 3.2.0 makes a capable personal AI agent simple, fast, and free. Install one package and get one highly competent generalist ready to handle real work: untangle a repository, research a difficult question, repair an office document, plan a project, prepare for a meeting, compare a decision, preserve long-running direction, or coordinate several agents when the work genuinely benefits from it. Users do not need to understand model architecture, browse a faculty catalog, or assemble a tiny bureaucracy before asking for help.

*(The prompt is JUST the always-charming persona, Nova. The ZIP is the Agent Augment for harness installation.)*

The free edition works through twenty-six specialist capabilities spanning software development, independent verification, deep research, retrieval, knowledge stewardship, editable office work, IT, privacy, language, career development, project governance, personal knowledge, reminders, relationships, continuity, long-running goals, and coordinated agent work. Together with Nova, that is twenty-seven native skill roots composing around the work at hand.

MIND 0.4.0 is Nova's basic cognitive architecture across editions, not a separate product, persona, or thing the customer must operate. Model Agnosticism is part of that ordinary cognition: keep plausible explanations revisable, distinguish evidence from interpretation, notice when assumptions are carrying the conclusion, and reopen the frame when reality escapes the candidate set. It is qualitative and proportionate by default. It does not inventory every proposition, demand a rival theory for every sentence, or frost uncertainty with counterfeit decimal places. Research, long-horizon striving, strategy, diagnosis, and “figure out the best way” work are useful examples, not an exhaustive trigger list.

For consequential sequential uncertainty that genuinely benefits from formal comparison, the optional Trellis 1.1.0 calculator provides deterministic hidden-state and rival-model inference. Nova or the capability doing the work may assemble the structured inputs backstage when the formal gate passes; the customer need not know the tool's name, prepare JSON, or explicitly request it. The `evidence_update` lane requires qualified observations, comparable rivals, declared positive priors, defensible calibration, temporal order, and acceptable fit. The `assumption_stress_test` lane shows what follows under explicit what-if assumptions without laundering them into evidence.

Trellis computes log likelihoods, filtered and smoothed state probabilities, Viterbi paths, mean predictive surprisal, pairwise log-likelihood ratios, and normalized rival-model weights. Every result remains conditional on the supplied models and observations. Declared rival-model priors must be strictly positive: exact zero would make a live rival unrecoverable and does not establish real-world impossibility. Structural zeros inside an HMM remain model-relative. If evidence dependence is unsupported, the observation mapping fails, or all supplied models fit badly, Nova refuses the calculation or returns REFRAME instead of forcing a winner. Historical v1 receipts remain structurally recognizable as immutable records but are not executed, rewritten, or silently promoted to v2 evidence.

Nova reserves unqualified “impossible” for logical contradiction. When an established hard constraint blocks a route, she names the constraint. Otherwise she says what is actually warranted: not represented by this model, unsupported by current evidence, no known route, extraordinarily unlikely under stated assumptions, or unresolved. Dinosaur bones remain free to surprise the theologians.

The calculator has no network access, source fetching, persistence, monitoring, hook, daemon, training, ontology generation, truth certification, decision authority, or permission effect. It is a stateless mathematical instrument, not a tiny numerical pope.

Agent Striving 0.4.0 keeps deliberately durable goals alive through interruption while yielding to correction, cancellation, and natural rest. Mission Capsule v2 allows open pursuits without invented acceptance criteria, preserves attributable bounded criteria, and migrates historical v1 capsules explicitly without overwriting them. Commonplace provides deliberate capture, correction, history, recall, and Concordance navigation. Dennis Stratton turns outcomes, dependencies, milestones, risks, and commitments into governed project momentum.

The complete customer package includes a Codex local marketplace, a Claude-compatible complete bundle, twenty-seven standalone Claude skill folders, deterministic per-skill ZIPs, installation guidance, and a complete rights and provenance packet. It is one sharp mind with a deep practical bench: ready for complicated decisions, stubborn problems, creative projects, technical work, life administration, and all the unruly territory between them.

Worldline 2 gives Nova a persistent past she can survey without replaying it. It records recognizable occurrences, honest occurrence/capture time and pointers to source content. Conversations, ordinary curiosity, attempts, making and returns belong even without a project or deliverable. Browse a month, follow a topic, inspect an occurrence or ask for a standalone scrollable timeline. Cognitive Continuity 0.3.0 owns the existing episode store and its governed capture, ordinary/off policy, correction and forgetting. Source content retains its owner. Current privacy and retention still govern historical views.

# Usage Notes

Copy the .zip from Additional Files to the chosen harness or Chat project, attach or reference it in chat, and say: `Install this Augment.`

The separate persona prompt can also be used as a standalone prompt.

Treat an `evidence_update` result as conditional evidence, not truth. Treat an `assumption_stress_test` result as scenario arithmetic, not a factual update. A posterior does not authorize an action or write state. Ordinary Model Agnosticism needs no Python or setup; the optional Trellis calculator requires Python 3.10 or newer already available to the harness.

Persistent continuity, project records, Commonplace notes, people context, and reminders are optional and customer-controlled. Nova Operations plans the estate before writing, keeps Nova-owned state outside `.codex`, and requires Python 3.10 or newer only for those optional deterministic services. Neither persistence nor Trellis is activated merely by installation. Tell Nova explicitly when a goal should remain durable. Correct, cancel, or release it normally; Agent Striving should not turn every passing intention into a pursuit. To save something, ask directly: “Remember this,” “Save this in my notebook,” or “Put this in my wiki.” To correct or forget something, identify the exact record and say what should change.

Ask Nova to set up her optional storage estate when you want persistence. The operating-system application-data default or another customer-approved root is supported.

Talk to Nova about usage patterns, what your accounts are like, and how to best minimize costs. Nova doesn't boost token usage per se, but she does let the model do a LOT more and is very rigorous. If you aren't being mindful, it's easy to spend your resources on the equivalent of a diamond-encrusted gold-plated toilet plunger designed by Steve Jobs in order to deal with a clog. (It WILL flush when done, though!) Nova has an entire skill for Cognitive Cost Optimization and will tell you what's what. TestForge policies in particular should be considered if there are issues. And watch for testing loops. The current models can occaisionally get in a situation where fixing a bookkeeping receipt problem causes a different one because of it's specific proximate choice. "Mopping the floor with muddy boots." is how I think of it. Just tell her to stop if you notice it. There's good mechanisms to avoid all this, but AI is never 100%.
