Nova at work

Show her the mess.

You do not route a miniature company of bots. Give Nova the outcome, the materials, and the limits. She keeps the work coherent and brings specialist depth forward only when it helps.

You

“This export test flakes in CI. Fix the real cause, preserve the public API, and tell me what proves the repair.”

Nova

Works in the repository, not around it

Nova inspects the code and tests, reproduces the failure if the environment permits, makes the smallest coherent change, and runs checks proportionate to the claim. If independent verification materially changes confidence, she brings it in. You receive the repair, the observed evidence, and any honest residual risk—not a lecture about internal routing.

You

“Tell me what changed in this policy, which parts affect our launch, and where the sources disagree.”

Nova

Separates current fact from confident noise

Nova checks current authoritative sources, searches your supplied documents where relevant, reconciles dates and contradictions, and cites the claims that matter. If new facts overturn the initial answer, the answer changes. Miraculous concept, really.

You

“Turn these notes and figures into an editable board deck and workbook. Keep the numbers consistent and audit the result.”

Nova

Creates the real files

Nova keeps source custody across formats, uses the host’s actual document and spreadsheet tools, renders what needs visual inspection, and returns editable artifacts. A file existing is not mistaken for a file being ready.

You

“Make this a long-term release goal, manage the project, and keep going. I’m going to bed.”

Nova

Keeps the future pulling on the present

Nova creates the durable goal only because you explicitly asked, gives the named project one canonical record, advances every safe authorized branch, and preserves the live edge through interruption. She does not turn missing fields into a bureaucracy garden or treat persistence as permission.

You

“Help me apply for this role using only what I can honestly support, then interview me one question at a time.”

Nova

Useful without inventing a better you

Nova maps the vacancy to real evidence, exposes gaps, writes materials that remain yours, and trains from the work you actually did. She can be charming without laundering fiction into confidence.
