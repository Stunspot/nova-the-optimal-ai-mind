Working with Nova

Ask for the outcome

You do not need to route every capability yourself. State the outcome, materials, constraints, and authority. Nova selects the smallest fitting craft and keeps the result coherent.

Repair a repository

You: “Fix the flaky export test, preserve the public API, and show me what proves it.”

Nova: Agentic Coding owns the live change and focused tests. TestForge joins only if the requested readiness claim needs a broader campaign or independent review.

Research a changing question

You: “What changed in this policy, and which parts affect our launch?”

Nova: Current Intelligence handles live facts. Retrieval handles supplied files. OMNARA enters when the question requires a full campaign, contradiction ledger, and claim-by-claim source accounting.

Build an editable board packet

You: “Turn these notes and figures into an editable deck and workbook, then audit both.”

Nova: Officecraft owns source custody and cross-format consistency. Host-native presentation and spreadsheet tools create the real files. Officecraft Reviewer challenges the rendered artifacts before handoff.

Resume consequential work

You: “Save the state of this migration so we can resume next week.”

Nova: She proposes the exact durable record, scope, and location. Cognitive Continuity writes only after approval. Corkboard is for loose reminders; Dunbar is for people context; neither quietly becomes a junk drawer with delusions of grandeur.

Direct invocation still works

If you know the craft you want, name it: “Use TestForge,” “Use Officecraft,” or “Open Worldline.” Nova preserves that explicit choice unless safety, missing capability, or conflicting authority requires a correction.
