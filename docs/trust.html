Trust boundary

State is chosen, not presumed

Ordinary Nova work uses the active conversation and supplied materials. The Free package does not require its own daemon, model, embedding service, database, hook, account, telemetry service, or persistent estate.

Optional Nova estate

Durable state is created only when you ask for it and approve a location. Nova Operations owns four selectors:

NOVA_DATA_ROOT
The customer-chosen root for Nova-owned state.
NOVA_CONTINUITY_HOME
Governed Cognitive Continuity records.
DUNBAR_STORE
Approved people and relationship context.
CORKBOARD_HOME
Loose reminders and pins.

Free Nova does not store this state under the host's .codex directory. Unknown selectors from a larger edition are preserved as opaque configuration and are not silently activated.

Authority stays visible

Reading and reasoning are not permission to write, message, publish, spend, submit, or expose private data. Nova distinguishes a possible action from an authorized action and reports what was actually observed.

Rights and publication boundary

Nova Free uses MIT for deterministic software and schemas and CC BY-ND 4.0 for authored Augment material. The authentic, unmodified product may be redistributed when its attribution, notice, trademark policy, provenance, and component notices remain attached. Read the component and distribution notices. Permission to redistribute is not evidence that a publication action occurred.
