What Nova can do

One agent. A suspicious amount of competence.

Nova is a genuine generalist, not a single-purpose chatbot wearing a larger hat. These are the jobs you can hand her—not a scavenger hunt through internal skill names.

01

Build, fix, and verify

Give Nova a repository, prompt, broken workflow, device problem, or office-file brief. She can inspect the real materials, make scoped changes, create editable deliverables, and bring in independent verification when the claim earns it.

Try saying:

“Fix this bug without changing the public API, then show me what proves it.”
“Turn these notes into an editable deck and workbook and check both.”
What powers this

Agentic Coding, Promptcraft, Software Verification, Verification Reviewer, Officecraft, Officecraft Reviewer, Beryl IT Tech, and IT Work Reviewer.

02

Research the world and your files

Nova can investigate changing public facts, search a supplied corpus, reconcile contradictions, preserve citations, and update guidance when new evidence changes the answer. She knows the difference between finding a file and understanding what it supports.

Try saying:

“What changed this week, what affects us, and which sources actually support that?”
“Search these documents for the answer and cite the exact passages.”
What powers this

Current Intelligence Observatory, Retrieval Intelligence, Retrieval Reviewer, AnswerLayer, Giles Knowledge Steward, and OMNARA Deep Research.

03

Run work that survives interruption

Nova can manage a named project, keep an authorized long-term pursuit alive, coordinate bounded parallel workers, and preserve only the state needed to resume. Projects stay projects; reminders stay reminders; people notes stay people notes. No memory soup.

Try saying:

“Set this up as a real project, keep going through the release, and let me go to bed.”
“Resume from the last unfinished edge. Do not reopen settled decisions.”
What powers this

Dennis Stratton Project Management, Agent Striving, Agent Swarm Orchestration, Cognitive Continuity, Nova Operations, Commonplace, Corkboard, and Dunbar.

04

Handle practical, human work

Prepare evidence-grounded applications, practice interviews one question at a time, translate with register and social nuance, protect sensitive information, and help organize what matters without inventing claims or quietly collecting your life.

Try saying:

“Match my real experience to this job and tell me where the evidence is thin.”
“Translate this so it sounds warm and natural, not like a municipal robot.”
What powers this

Job Application Builder, Interview Trainer, Lex Foster Language Companion, and Privacy Redline.

And the thinking?

MIND is built in.

Seventeen Faculty Cores help Nova decide, plan, interpret, verify, create, measure, time, and adapt. They are depth inside the agent—not seventeen more bots you must configure or remember. The architecture is available for inspection; the customer experience is simply Nova being unusually good at the work.
