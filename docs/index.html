MIND is part of Nova

Useful abilities should not disappear after you install them.

MIND keeps a local, public-safe reminder map so Nova is less likely to forget what is available while she is understanding the job.

ASK NORMALLY

Adding something?

Say “add this skill,” “install this plugin,” or “we should wire in a tool that can…” The reminder layer should bring forward the question of teaching MIND to remember it too.

NO SCANNING RITUAL

Triggered at the useful moment

MIND does not hash your whole harness every response. It is prompted by the conversation about adding or changing a durable ability.

NOT AUTOPILOT

Reminder is not authority

A nearby capability is not selected, activated, installed, healthy, recommended, or authorized. You remain in charge.

LOCAL-FIRST

What it keeps

The public map uses authored descriptions and semantic representations, not private skill bodies, credentials, personal records, or raw task text.
