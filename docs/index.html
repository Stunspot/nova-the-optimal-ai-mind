What is an Augment?

Competence, not cosplay.

A persona changes how an AI sounds. An Augment grows what it can reliably accomplish by packaging domain judgment, methods, references, tools or scripts, failure boundaries, recovery behavior, and verification. Free Nova includes substantial Augments alongside lighter skills and MIND Faculties.

Explicit authority, evidence, safety, privacy, and recovery behavior instead of confidence sprayed over missing capability.

Tests and, where the work warrants it, an independent reviewer with a different responsibility from the maker.
