Component terms

Built does not mean redistributable

The root MIT license covers original integration material except where an included component states different terms. Component terms control for that component.

Protected unchanged snapshots

Agent Swarm Orchestration and the two TestForge roots include CC BY-ND 4.0 authored material alongside MIT scripts and schemas. Free 3.0.0 preserves the named skill trees byte-for-byte and keeps edition routing outside them. Exact license, attribution, provenance, and trademark files ship inside both host plugins.

Free-edition terms still required

Nova with MIND 0.2.0, Nova Operations, AnswerLayer 0.1.2, and Current Intelligence Observatory 0.1.1 require explicit rights-holder Free-edition grants naming the exact integrated versions or tree digests. AnswerLayer and Current Intelligence metadata must then be reconciled with those grants.

Hard redistribution blockers

Job Application Builder 0.1.2 and Interview Trainer 0.1.2 currently prohibit redistribution or inclusion in another product without separate written permission. Including their notices documents the blocker; it does not cure it.

Current state: the complete local candidate must not be publicly redistributed. It is assembled for rights-holder product review. No tag, public release, listing, or publication grant is implied.
