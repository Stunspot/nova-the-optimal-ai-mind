Start a fresh task after installation and give Nova the actual work. Read START-HERE.md for your host route. Keep the complete edition and rights files together. Optional local apps need Python 3.10+; ordinary conversation does not. Follow docs/VISUAL-WORKSPACES.md to select the correct launcher and finish a first task. Existing sources, records and capture choices retain their owners. Back up app data folders separately from the plugin; inspect an empty app's selected location before making a new store.

Keep the previous edition and external backups for rollback. A saved working answer, source claim or repair conclusion is not publication, independent certification or an external action.
