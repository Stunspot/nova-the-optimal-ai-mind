## Free-edition clarification required

Nova with MIND 0.2.0 and Nova Operations were imported from proprietary construction sources and need an explicit Free-edition grant naming the integrated version or tree digest. AnswerLayer 0.1.2 currently says proprietary. Current Intelligence Observatory 0.1.1 currently says it is not supplied for complete-product redistribution and grants only a narrower attributed subset.

An approved grant must be followed by reconciliation of AnswerLayer and Current Intelligence package metadata. Naming the Beryl pair and Privacy Redline in that grant would also remove their residual chain-of-title ambiguity.

## Hard blockers

Job Application Builder 0.1.2 and Interview Trainer 0.1.2 expressly prohibit redistribution or inclusion in another product without separate written permission. Their exact license-status and provenance files are included in both host plugins. Including those notices records the blocker; it does not cure it.

Before any public copy, tag marketed as a release, GitHub release asset, directory submission, or customer distribution, obtain authorized written grants covering all six unresolved components and reconcile the conflicting component metadata. Then rerun the license, Hesperos, TestForge, package, and fresh-host gates against the exact resulting revision.
